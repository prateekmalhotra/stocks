### 2. CRITICAL RED-TEAM VULNERABILITIES & STRESS FACTORS

Audit analysis reveals three critical operational and structural vulnerabilities that directly threaten the high-growth narrative priced into today’s stock price:

#### **Vulnerability 1: Top-of-Funnel Search Referral Volatility (Google SGE Risk)**
* **Fact-Audit Warning:** In the Q2 2026 earnings call, management explicitly cited "choppy" top-of-funnel referral traffic resulting from search engine algorithm shifts and Google AI Overviews.
* **Structural Threat:** Over 50% of Reddit's logged-out visitors arrive via organic Google searches. As search engines deploy AI-synthesized answer blocks directly on search result pages, top-of-funnel click-through volume to Reddit threads faces structural decay, risking top-of-funnel user acquisition.

#### **Vulnerability 2: Data Licensing Contract Renewal Horizon (2026/2027)**
* **Concentration Risk:** Data licensing and subscriptions under "Other Revenue" generated **$43.0M in Q2 2026** (5.3% of revenue) and **$153.5M LTM**.
* **Renewal Window:** Primary multi-year data access agreements with Google and OpenAI (signed in 2024) face contract renewal negotiations in late 2026 and early 2027.
* **Pricing Threat:** If frontier LLM developers transition heavily toward synthetic training data or self-hosted web crawlers, Reddit’s pricing power during contract renegotiations could compress, preventing the revenue scale modeled in bull-case scenarios.

#### **Vulnerability 3: Share Price Misalignment with Fundamental Owner Earnings**
* **SBC Cash Flow Distortion:** Wall Street consensus frequently evaluates Reddit on Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA ($343.0M in Q2 2026). However, adding back Stock-Based Compensation ($310.0M LTM) ignores shareholder dilution.
* **True Owner Earnings Realities:** Deducting SBC as a cash-equivalent equity expense reduces LTM GAAP Operating Cash Flow ($1,051.0M) to a Core Baseline Owner Earnings (OE₀) of **$624.0M**.
