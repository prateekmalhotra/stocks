### Critical Vulnerabilities & Blindspots to Fix

#### 1. The Terminal Value Flaw in the Bear Case (The "Perpetual Growth" Trap)
In **Story 3 (Defensive Stress / Downside)**, you model a **-12.19% annual contraction** in Owner Earnings over 5 years down to $105M, but you still assign a **+2.00% perpetual terminal growth rate** with a 9.5% discount rate (a 7.5% capitalization divisor, implying a **13.3x terminal cash flow multiple**).
* **The Error:** A business suffering from permanent dating app fatigue, payer resistance, and structural FX headwinds will not suddenly pivot to GDP-level perpetual growth (+2.0%) in Year 6. 
* **The Correction:** In a structural decline scenario, you should apply either:
  * A **negative terminal growth rate** (e.g., -2.0% to -4.0%), or
  * A **terminal exit multiple** reflecting distressed/decaying software assets (e.g., **4.0x–5.5x Owner Earnings**).
* *Impact:* At a 5.0x terminal multiple on $105M OE ($525M terminal value discounted back), plus the PV of 5-year cash flows ($545M), Enterprise Value drops to ~$879M ($6.67/sh). After deducting net debt of -$2.56/sh, **true Bear Case intrinsic value lands closer to $4.10/share**—much closer to current price reality.

#### 2. Narrative vs. Reality on the "Women-First" Moat
* The thesis characterizes the "women make the first move" constraint as Bumble’s primary durable economic moat.
* **The Reality:** Bumble had to significantly soften this constraint (introducing **"Opening Moves"** to allow men to respond first to preset prompts) because female users reported overwhelming friction, conversational fatigue, and cognitive load from initiating every interaction.
* **Refinement:** Frame "women-first" not as an unyielding structural moat, but as an evolving **brand safety halo** that is actively undergoing product re-engineering to reduce female user friction.

#### 3. Underestimating Gen Z Behavioral Shifts & Platform Burnout
The thesis treats dating app fatigue primarily as a cyclical headwind in Story 3. Evidence across the entire online dating vertical (Match Group/Tinder, Bumble, Badoo) suggests a deeper structural shift:
* **Gamification Fatigue:** Gen Z cohorts exhibit lower conversion-to-paid rates, higher uninstall velocities, and an increasing aversion to subscription paywalls for core matching mechanics.
* **Off-App Matchmaking & Social Discovery:** Informal social discovery (Instagram, TikTok, IRL social running clubs/mixers) is siphoning high-intent users away from traditional swiping apps.
* **CAC Escalation:** When word-of-mouth organic loops break down, performance marketing costs rise faster than top-line revenue can expand, accelerating operating deleverage.

#### 4. Balance Sheet & Refinancing Squeeze in a Downside Regime
* In Story 3, revenue drops to $790M and Adjusted EBITDA compresses to 23% (~$181M EBITDA). 
* Against **$451M in funded debt**, Net Leverage climbs toward **2.5x–3.0x EBITDA**. While not immediately insolvent, in a higher-for-longer interest rate environment, refinancing this term loan in 2030 would incur higher credit spreads, absorbing a larger portion of the $105M Owner Earnings than modeled.
