### Detailed Forensic Corrections:

1. **FCFF vs. FCFE Double-Counting Correction (Flaw #1):**
   * *Thesis Error:* Started from GAAP OCF ($7.31B)—which is a levered, post-interest cash flow—and subsequently subtracted $7.11B in funded debt and $2.50B in lease liabilities on the balance sheet.
   * *Recalibration:* Added back post-tax interest expense (+$0.35B) to establish true **Unlevered Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF)** baseline before deducting balance sheet obligations.
2. **Elimination of Double Working Capital Penalty (Flaw #2):**
   * *Thesis Error:* Deducted a $0.75B annual OCF haircut *and* quarantined $5.58B in balance sheet cash (3.0% of revenue) for working capital.
   * *Recalibration:* JD operates a direct retail model with a negative working capital cycle (collecting cash from consumers long before paying vendors via 30–60 day payables). Quarantining cash while shaving OCF double-penalizes the business. The balance sheet quarantine was removed.
3. **Share Count & Buyback Synchronization (Flaw #3):**
   * *Thesis Error:* Held ADS count static at 1,336M while evaluating cash outflows.
   * *Recalibration:* Adjusted diluted ADS count down to **1,302 million** to reflect the $1.0B in repurchases (2.5% of total shares) executed in H1 2026 under the active $5.0B authorization.
4. **Correction of Stock-Based Compensation Accounting (Flaw #4):**
   * *Thesis Error:* Deducted 100% of non-cash SBC ($0.60B) directly from cash flow while ignoring share repurchases.
   * *Recalibration:* SBC is non-cash. Its true economic cost is offset by cash spent on share buybacks to prevent dilution. Reduced SBC cash deduction to $0.30B to net out anti-dilutive buyback efficiency.
5. **Re-incorporation of New Businesses Loss Realities (Flaw #5):**
   * *Thesis Error:* Claimed New Businesses losses "narrowed sharply," omitting the **RMB 47.03B ($6.72B USD)** operating loss in FY2025.
   * *Recalibration:* Accounted for quarterly capital deployment swings in food delivery, establishing an ongoing annualized segment burn expectation of RMB 15B–20B until full rationalization.

1. **Macro & Category Concentration Risk:** Electronics and home appliances account for 53% of merchandise revenues. Continued macro softness in domestic consumer discretionary spend or upstream commodity cost inflation directly depresses top-line growth (-11.8% in Q2 2026).
2. **Aggressive Domestic Price Competition:** Competitors (PDD Holdings, Alibaba's Taobao/Tmall, Douyin) continue to wage structural low-price wars. While JD relies on supply chain cost efficiency rather than raw subsidies, pricing pressure forces JD Retail to increase marketing spend or trim marketplace take-rates to protect market share.
3. **VIE Structure & Cross-Border Capital Controls:** JD operates through Variable Interest Entities (VIEs) in Mainland China. Cash held onshore face regulatory approval and a 5%–10% withholding tax upon upstream cross-border dividend distribution. We have explicitly built a **$1.50B tax friction haircut** into our Net Cash calculation to address this.
4. **Execution Drag from On-Demand Delivery & Overseas Expansion:** While New Businesses losses narrowed in Q2 2026, re-entering aggressive food delivery subsidy cycles or over-expanding European Joybuy assets could drain cash flow and delay consolidation goals.
