### 2. Accounting & Valuation Nuances

#### A. Lease Liability Double-Counting Nuance (ASC 842)
* You deduct **$4.88B in ASC 842 operating lease liabilities** directly from Gross Cash to arrive at Surplus Cash. 
* *Accounting Note:* Under US GAAP, operating lease payments (rent) are already deducted inside Operating Cash Flow (OCF). Deducting the capitalized present value of lease liabilities from balance sheet cash while simultaneously using OCF to capitalized Owner Earnings can result in a **minor double-counting of lease obligations**.
* *Recommendation:* Clarify that this deduction is an intentional, ultra-conservative "balance-sheet liquidation haircut" rather than standard enterprise valuation accounting, or shift lease payments entirely into debt/EV adjustments.
