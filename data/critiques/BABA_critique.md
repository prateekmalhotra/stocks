### 1. Severe Owner Cash Flow Dilution & Heavy CapEx Drag
* **Trough Cash Conversion:** GAAP Operating Cash Flow plunged to $\$10,541\text{M}$ in FY 2026 due to quick commerce inventory expansion and merchant subsidy advances. Subtracting $\$4,841\text{M}$ in Maintenance CapEx (grounded in Form 20-F Note 1 server useful lives of 5–6 years) and $\$2,050\text{M}$ in Stock-Based Compensation yields a trough **Core Owner Earnings ($\text{OE}_0$) of just $\$3,181\text{M}$ (\$1.33/ADS)**.
* **Multi-Year Capital Sink:** Management's commitment to spend **$> \text{RMB } 380\text{B}$ ($\$53\text{B}+$)** over 3 years on AI hardware implies growth CapEx will absorb the vast majority of operating cash flows through FY 2028.

### 2. Domestic Retail Competition & Capped Pricing Power
* **Structural Market Share Loss:** Persistent GMV theft by **Pinduoduo (PDD)** in value segments and **ByteDance (Douyin E-Commerce)** in live commerce ($>\text{RMB } 3.0\text{T}$ GMV) limits BABA's pricing power.
* **Monetization Treadmill:** BABA's 0.6% software service fee introduction has been largely offset by merchant tool subsidies and reduced ad rates to prevent seller migration. CMR growth (+1% YoY in Q3 FY2026) continues to lag GMV growth.

### 3. Cross-Border Supply Chain & Regulatory Tariff Shock
* **US De Minimis Repeal:** Executive Order 14256 and legislative repeal of Section 321 eliminated duty-free import status for parcels under \$800, subjecting AliExpress Choice shipments to formal customs entries and $\ge 7.5\%$ Section 301 tariffs. Total US de minimis import volume contracted **-28% YoY** in 2025.
* **EU Customs Duty Mandate:** Effective July 1, 2026, the EU institutes a mandatory **€3 flat customs duty per parcel** under €150, designating marketplaces as legal customs debtors. This directly impairs AIDC unit economics and delays segment profitability.

### 4. Currency Depreciation & Governance Structures
* **FX Headwind:** A 14% weakening of the Renminbi (from RMB 7.23 to RMB 8.25 / \$1.00 USD) depresses USD-denominated owner earnings by $\sim 12.4\%$, creating a persistent translation drag.
* **VIE Structural Friction:** Upstream cash transfers from mainland China operating entities through WFOEs remain subject to a **5%–10% statutory dividend withholding tax**, restricting seamless capital deployment to US ADS holders.
