### A. Quant Audit Forensic Corrections
1. **Section 1 Table 2 Margin Calculation Error:**

2. **Reverse DCF Matrix Terminal Multiple Slippage:**
   * *Thesis Error:* The thesis claimed that at a 10.5% hurdle rate, the market-implied growth requirement jumps to 13.4% CAGR (and 16.5% at 11.5%).
   * *Audit Correction:* Python modeling proves the thesis author silently compressed terminal exit multiples down to 12.0x and 10.0x inside those cells. Holding the terminal multiple constant at 13.6x, a 10.5% hurdle rate requires only **10.4% annual growth** (and 11.5% requires **11.4% annual growth**).
3. **Unlinked Balance Sheet Cash Surplus Plug:**
   * *Thesis Error:* The thesis applied a **+$51.28/share (+$2,600M)** net cash surplus adjustment to the Bull Case while assigning $0.00/share to the Base Case without a balance sheet line-item bridge.
   * *Audit Correction:* Balance sheet cash ($X) less debt ($Y) is an objective present-day asset. If unencumbered cash is credited in the Bull Case, applying it consistently to the Base Case elevates Base Case Intrinsic Value from **$2,503.79 to $2,555.07 / ADS**.
