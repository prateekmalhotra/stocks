### **3. Gross Margin Drag & GPU Depreciation Squeeze**
Microsoft Cloud gross margins dropped from **72% historically to 65% in Q4 FY26**, with Q1 FY27 guided down to **~64%**. Management confirmed **~100 bps of operating margin compression in FY27**. 
* **Accounting Mismatch Warning:** Although Microsoft extended statutory server depreciation to 6 years in SEC Form 10-K disclosures, actual high-performance AI accelerators (GPUs) face physical and technical obsolescence within **3 to 4 years**. Shortening depreciation schedules to reflect real-world hardware lifecycles increases Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) and depresses operating income.

### **4. Customer & Contract Concentration (OpenAI Exposure)**
Commercial Remaining Performance Obligations (RPO) grew **84% YoY to $678B**. However, **excluding OpenAI commitments, commercial RPO grew 25% YoY**. Microsoft’s cloud growth is heavily reliant on OpenAI’s ongoing solvency, model dominance, and infrastructure commitments.

### **5. Consumer Hardware Contraction & Supply Chain Tariff Exposure**
More Personal Computing (MPC) remains a persistent drag on returns:
* **Xbox Hardware Slump:** Xbox console revenues fell **29%–31% YoY** in recent quarters. Games software growth in FY24/FY25 was artificially inflated by the **$68.7B Activision Blizzard acquisition**. With inorganic gains now lapped, organic gaming demand is contracting.
* **Tariff Friction:** Manufacturing hubs in China and Vietnam face supply chain tariffs. Unlike smartphones, gaming consoles lack broad tariff exemptions, compressing hardware margins. Price hikes on Xbox Series X/S (up to $800 in select markets) have dampened unit volumes relative to competitors.
