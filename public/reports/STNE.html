Section 1: The Premise of the Company

StoneCo Ltd. (NASDAQ: STNE) operates as a leading financial technology and merchant operating engine in Brazil, delivering an integrated platform of payment processing, digital banking solutions, working-capital financing, and business tools to micro, small, and medium-sized businesses (MSMBs). Founded on a proprietary hyper-local distribution network known as "Stone Hubs," the enterprise bypasses legacy Brazilian banking friction by combining localized sales, rapid physical point-of-sale (POS) terminal deployment, direct client service, and proprietary financial infrastructure.

The Central Investment Thesis: At ~$9.16/share, Mr. Market is pricing StoneCo at an extreme discount (6.54x Mid-Cycle Owner Earnings of $1.40/share), reflecting market skepticism regarding earnings durability amidst elevated Brazilian benchmark interest rates (Selic at 10.5%+) and rising credit loss provisions on 2025 credit vintages. Following the divestment of Linx for R$ 3.1B (~$599M USD) and capital return via special dividends and buybacks, StoneCo is a cleaner financial engine where merchant banking deposit growth and collateralized credit cross-sell offer an asymmetric risk/reward even under conservative working-capital assumptions.

1. Core Business Model & Unit Monetization Engine (Post-Linx Clean Engine)

StoneCo’s unit monetization relies on a four-tier merchant financial operating flywheel:

  • Merchant Acquiring & PIX Monetization: StoneCo collects a Net Merchant Discount Rate (Net MDR of 1.0%–1.2%) on card transaction volume. Crucially, StoneCo has successfully monetized Brazil Central Bank's PIX QR Code transactions at yields comparable to debit rails while using PIX to drive active digital banking adoption (3.7M+ banking accounts).
  • Receivables Prepayment (Pre-pagamento de Recebíveis): Brazilian merchants face 30-day settlement windows. StoneCo provides upfront liquidity via receivables discounting, earning a substantial spread above the benchmark Selic rate.
  • Digital Banking & Float Monetization: StoneCo captures low-cost merchant deposits (exceeding R$ 11.1B / ~$2.0B USD), generating net interest margin float and recurring account servicing fees.
  • Collateralized Merchant Credit: Revolving credit lines and working-capital loans are automatically collected as a percentage of daily card processing settlement flow, structurally lowering default risk relative to uncollateralized lending.
  • Strategic Divestment of Linx: The sale of Linx to TOTVS closed in early 2026 for R$ 3.1 billion (~$599M USD), eliminating software distractions, funding a $2.53/share special dividend in April 2026, and refocusing 100% of capital on high-ROIC financial services.

2. Brazilian Macro Headwinds: Selic Interest Rates & Credit Provisioning Dynamics

  • Selic Benchmark Interest Rates (10.5%–11.5%+): High central bank interest rates increase funding costs for receivables discounting and tighten merchant cash flows, compressing gross margin expansion.
  • Credit Portfolio Risk & 90+ Day NPLs: Working capital credit expansion carries credit cycle risk. In 1H 2026, non-performing loans (NPLs) on older 2025 credit vintages elevated the cost of risk (provision charges consumed ~21.5% of gross financial income). Controlling NPLs below 6.0% is the critical operational variable for earnings durability.
  • Q2 2026 Operational Reality: Q2 revenue reached R$ 3.59B (+2.5% YoY), gross profit was flat, and adjusted net income contracted -2.6% YoY, confirming that the market is pricing earnings deceleration rather than irrational panic.

3. Governance, Executive Control & Insider Alignment

  • Dual-Class Voting Structure: Class A common shares carry 1 vote per share, while Class B common shares carry 10 votes per share, held by founding partners (including André Street), providing anti-takeover protection.
  • C-Suite Transition (March 2026): Former CEO Pedro Zinner completed his planned 3-year mandate and became Chairman of the Board. Mateus Scherer Schwening (former CFO and architect of Stone's banking engine) was appointed CEO, while Diego Salgado was named CFO.
  • Insider Buying: CFO Diego Salgado purchased +22,490 shares (+$217K at $9.66) in the open market following the Q1 results.

Path 1: Disciplined MSMB Hub Penetration & Banking Cross-Sell (50% Weight)

Operational Summary: StoneCo delivers steady operational continuation across Brazilian merchant hubs. Year-5 Owner Earnings reach $4.20 per share (+24.6% CAGR). Applying a 14.0x terminal multiple produces a 5-year target price of $58.80 (Present Fair Value: $37.35).
