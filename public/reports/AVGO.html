01

What the Market is Pricing In

Mr. Market is currently pricing Broadcom Inc. (AVGO) at $368.45 per share, representing a rich 46.1x P/OE multiple, an EV/OE of 44.8x, and a modest 2.2% owner cash yield. This valuation reflects market expectations of flawless execution across two massive growth drivers: complete monopoly power in custom AI application-specific integrated circuits (ASICs) for cloud hyperscalers, and permanent margin extraction from the $69 billion VMware acquisition. Investors paying today's multiple are pricing in smooth multi-year scaling in custom silicon without accounting for structural customer concentration risks or non-AI inventory headwinds.

Skeptics, on the other hand, view the elevated market price as vulnerable to multiple compression. Sell-side adjustments, such as Goldman Sachs removing AVGO from its Conviction List and Macquarie downgrading the stock, highlight rising risks around customer diversification. Over half of Broadcom's projected AI semiconductor expansion rests on a small handful of cloud giants like Google, Meta, and ByteDance. As Google actively introduces second-source custom silicon partners like MediaTek (TPU v8 Zebrafish/Humufish) and Marvell (expanded ASIC and attach agreement with a $12.2B warrant structure) to lower procurement costs, and as enterprise clients revolt against aggressive VMware subscription pricing, skeptics argue that Broadcom's cash generation faces margin compression and deceleration, leaving the current 46.1x P/OE multiple deeply exposed.
