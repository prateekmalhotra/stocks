Section 1: Company Overview & Audited Financial Baseline

AppLovin Corporation (NASDAQ: APP) operates a global software infrastructure platform that provides AI-powered marketing, monetization, and measurement solutions for digital advertisers and app developers. Headquartered in Palo Alto, California, the enterprise acts as an essential tollbridge in the programmatic digital advertising ecosystem. Its core technology platform—anchored by the AXON 2.0 machine learning engine, MAX supply-side mediation platform, AppDiscovery demand-side engine, and Adjust attribution analytics software—enables real-time algorithmic ad placement and user acquisition optimization across mobile applications, web e-commerce, and connected television (CTV).

In June 2025, AppLovin completed a transformative strategic divestiture of its internal mobile games portfolio (Apps segment) to Tripledot Studios for $900 million in total consideration ($500 million cash + $400 million equity). This eliminated structural conflicts of interest where AppLovin operated both as an ad infrastructure provider for third-party publishers and as a competing game developer. Today, AppLovin stands as a pure-play, high-margin AI advertising software engine with industry-leading operational leverage, generating ~80%+ Adjusted EBITDA margins.

Current Reporting Period: FY 2025 / Q2 2026 LTM (Trailing Twelve Months ended June 30, 2026).

Core Business Machine, Moat & Unit Economics

Primary Economic Moat Rating: [Wide Moat]

AppLovin’s economic moat stems from powerful self-reinforcing network effects, high customer switching costs, and proprietary algorithmic advantage:

  • AXON 2.0 AI Engine & Data Flywheel: AXON 2.0 processes trillions of real-time signals across thousands of mobile apps to predict user conversion probabilities and return on ad spend (ROAS). As more advertisers spend on AppDiscovery, the engine receives more behavioral outcome data, improving predictive accuracy, lowering customer acquisition costs, and attracting further ad spend.
  • MAX Mediation Monopoly & Supply-Side Scale: MAX sits as the premier supply-side mediation tool, conducting real-time header bidding auctions for top mobile game publishers. By mediating approximately 70% of top mobile gaming ad inventory, AppLovin enjoys unassailable visibility into publisher supply, forcing demand partners to integrate directly with its auctions.
  • Expansion Beyond Gaming into Web & E-Commerce: In mid-2026, AppLovin launched its self-serve AXON advertising platform for web e-commerce advertisers, extending its AI bidding engine from native mobile gaming into general digital commerce.

Buffett & Munger Pricing Power & Inflation Resistance Audit

Pricing Power Classification: [Strong Pricing Power]

AppLovin does not rely on list price increases or manual contract negotiations. Instead, its dynamic real-time auction engine automatically captures pricing gains based on the quantifiable Return on Investment (ROI) delivered to advertisers. When AXON improves targeting accuracy, advertisers experience higher conversion rates and automatically bid up prices per install/impression. In FY 2025, net revenue per installation surged 72% year-over-year, and expanded another 58% year-over-year in Q2 2026, illustrating immense value surplus creation.
