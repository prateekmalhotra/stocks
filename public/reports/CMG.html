01

What the Market is Pricing In

At a current market price of $37.22 (28.6x P/OE, 27.6x EV/OE, 3.5% Owner Yield), skeptics argue that Chipotle Mexican Grill, Inc. is priced for perfection, assuming frictionless unit-reinvestment compounding that overlooks underlying organic volume stagnation. The market is increasingly concerned that top-line performance is being artificially sustained through aggressive new unit store rollouts rather than organic four-wall restaurant productivity. With same-store transaction comps fluctuating wildly and dropping into negative territory in recent quarters (-2.5% in Q4 2025), sell-side consensus (Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan) warns that low-to-middle income consumers are experiencing severe menu price fatigue and cumulative inflation pushback, leaving CMG unable to pass along further price hikes without triggering severe traffic loss.

Furthermore, Mr. Market is pricing in persistent margin compression stemming from sticky input cost inflation and operational friction. Escalating beef prices, freight tariffs, elevated store hourly wages, and portion-size remediation efforts are squeezing restaurant-level operating margins down from peak 27%+ levels toward 24%–25%. Sell-side bear notes highlight that without accelerating traffic growth, the core investment asymmetry centers on whether CMG is experiencing a temporary operational soft patch or if its $37.22 valuation leaves zero margin of safety against structural unit cannibalization and multiple compression toward historical fast-casual industry norms.

How Things Are Going Now

In operational reality, Chipotle continues to execute as a high-volume fast-casual compounder, though sequential results highlight clear divergence between store expansion and same-store volume trends. Over the past four quarters, total system revenue expanded from $3,003.4M in Q3 2025 to $3,348.6M in Q2 2026, translating to a current annualized run-rate of $13,394.4M across 4,201 total systemwide company-owned and partner-operated restaurants. Same-store sales growth has fluctuated significantly—moving from +0.3% in Q3 2025 down to -2.5% in Q4 2025, before recovering mildly to +0.5% in Q1 2026 and +2.2% in Q2 2026 (driven by +1.0% transaction growth and +1.2% check size).

Restaurant-level operating margins have moderated to 25.2% in Q2 2026 (down 220 bps YoY from 27.4%), impacted by beef and freight cost inflation alongside higher hourly store labor expenses. Digital channel sales remain strong at 38.3% of total revenue ($1.28B in Q2 2026) anchored by average unit volumes (AUVs) running at ~$3.2M. The company maintains a fortress balance sheet with zero long-term funded debt and $1,850.0M in net cash ($1.37/share). Capital allocation remains focused on unit expansion (~350–370 openings in FY26, ~80% featuring Chipotlanes) and share repurchases, with $630.7M deployed in buybacks during Q2 2026 alone at an average price of $32.55.
