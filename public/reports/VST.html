01

What the Market is Pricing In

At the current valuation of $136.21 (17.0x P/OE, 15.8x EV/OE), Mr. Market is debating whether Vistra's current earnings power represents an unsustainable cyclical peak in merchant power prices or a permanent structural step-change. Skeptics are pricing in potential risks of regulatory intervention by FERC and regional RTOs regarding behind-the-meter data center co-location tariffs, forward spark spread compression in ERCOT due to rapid solar and battery additions, and eventual capacity price normalization across PJM. They view recent windfall earnings as a temporary tight-grid phenomenon that will compress back to historical utility mid-cycle levels.

However, this skepticism creates a compelling core asymmetry. Market pricing overlooks Vistra's multi-year structural transition into a contracted infrastructure compounder. Powered by long-term 20-year nuclear PPAs with hyperscalers like AWS and Meta, the structural downside protection of the IRA Section 45U PTC price floor ($43.75/MWh), and an aggressive, highly accretive capital allocation framework that systematically reduces share count, Vistra is positioned for sustained per-share cash flow growth rather than terminal decay.

How Things Are Going Now

Vistra is delivering robust operational performance across its integrated generation and retail platform. Sequential generation output across PJM and ERCOT reached 50.5 TWh in recent periods, supported by commercial availability exceeding 97.0% during summer peak demand. Realized power prices and spark spreads have expanded YoY, driven by record PJM capacity auction clearing prices and active commercial optimization, while the retail division (~5 million customer equivalents) continues to capture stable gross margins and act as a natural structural hedge against wholesale volatility.

Operational momentum is anchored by high-value long-term commercial wins, including 20-year baseload nuclear PPAs with Amazon Web Services and Meta across the Comanche Peak and PJM nuclear fleets. Management has secured Investment Grade balance sheet status (BBB-/Baa3), providing lower capital costs and liquidity flexibility.

Furthermore, capital allocation remains exceptionally disciplined. Vistra has repurchased over 30% of its outstanding share count since late 2021 at highly accretive average prices, driving compounding per-share Owner Earnings and cash conversion (~60% FCFbG conversion from Adjusted EBITDA) that significantly outpaces traditional utility peers.
