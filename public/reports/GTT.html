01

What the Market is Pricing In

At the current market price of $241.52, Gaztransport & Technigaz (GTT) is valued at 21.8x P/OE and 21.0x EV/OE against an annualized revenue run-rate of $848.0M. Mr. Market is caught between acknowledging GTT's near-monopolistic 90%+ global market share in cryogenic membrane containment systems for liquefied natural gas (LNG) carriers and pricing in fear that the current shipbuilding cycle is touching an operational peak. The prevailing skeptical thesis assumes that after an unprecedented surge in LNG carrier orders driven by European energy security diversification and Asian demand, shipyard capacity saturation and project financing delays will trigger an inevitable order drought.

Consequently, the market is pricing GTT as an elite but late-cycle royalty machine. Skeptics argue that while current Owner Earnings of $11.08 per share ($410.8M total) are exceptionally cash-generative and backed by $8.74 per share in net cash ($324M total), future growth will decelerate as shipyards deliver the existing order backlog without replenishing it at the previous pace. The valuation disconnect lies between investors who view GTT's revenue as an irreplaceable technological toll on global gas transport and those who fear that shipyard slot saturation and long-term decarbonization policies will cap terminal growth.
