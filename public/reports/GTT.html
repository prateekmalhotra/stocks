01

What the Market is Pricing In

At $250.30 per share (~€216.80), Mr. Market is pricing Gaztransport & Technigaz (GTT) at 23.8x normalized Owner Earnings and 22.9x Enterprise Value to Owner Earnings. This premium multiple reflects widespread institutional recognition of GTT's virtual global monopoly in cryogenic membrane containment systems for LNG carriers, where it commands over 90% market share across tier-1 South Korean and Chinese shipyards.

However, prevailing market skepticism centers on the durability of the current mega-order cycle. Wall Street bears and cyclical skeptics argue that record order intakes between 2022 and 2025—triggered by the European energy crisis and Qatar's North Field expansion—represent an unsustainable cyclical peak. With Asian shipyard delivery slots largely booked through 2028–2030, skeptics anticipate an inevitable order intake deceleration as liquefaction project sanctioning normalizes. Additionally, bears point to ongoing antitrust scrutiny by European competition authorities and long-term substitute containment technologies under R&D in China as potential catalysts for structural royalty take-rate erosion.

The central debate for investors is whether GTT is an irreplaceable global tollbooth positioned to harvest multi-year licensing royalties from a contracted 297+ vessel backlog and emerging LNG-as-fuel markets, or a cyclical engineering firm whose earnings power will plateau as global LNG carrier additions meet medium-term fleet capacity.

Trailing 12-month revenue stands at $874.1M, with the latest quarter delivering $212.3M, establishing a solid annualized run-rate of $849.3M. Unit volumes and backlog visibility remain exceptionally strong. Cash generation and balance sheet strength are immaculate.
