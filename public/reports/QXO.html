What the Market is Pricing In

Investors are pricing in significant execution risk around management's ability to extract projected synergies and deploy centralized AI dynamic pricing across a fragmented branch footprint.

The presence of convertible preferred stock, deeply in-the-money warrants held by executive leadership, and forced stock issuance from mega-acquisitions has expanded the diluted share denominator rapidly. Equity markets are questioning whether near-term operating cash flows can service the $6.03B+ debt stack and preferred dividend obligations, forcing the stock to trade at a substantial discount to platform roll-up narratives.
