What the Market is Pricing In

At the current share price of $72.57 (12.5x P/OE, 11.2x EV/OE), Mr. Market is pricing in severe execution skepticism surrounding Rocket Lab's launch frequency, operational cost structure, and transition into medium-lift launch capabilities. The prevailing bear thesis highlights sequential deceleration in small-lift launch revenue alongside gross margin compression in Space Systems as lower-margin acquired hardware (Mynaric and Motiv) dilutes GAAP gross margins down toward the 29% to 31% range.

Skepticism centers on potential timeline slippage for the medium-lift Neutron launch vehicle. Wall Street bears fear that pushing inaugural launch schedules into late 2026/2027 risks missing crucial early qualification windows for lucrative National Security Space Launch (NSSL) Phase 3 Lane 1 defense contracts. Combined with aggressive rideshare pricing pressure from SpaceX's Falcon 9 program, the market is discounting current cash flows as if Rocket Lab will remain a capital-intensive, lower-margin defense subcontractor rather than scaling into a high-margin dual-engine space infrastructure platform.
