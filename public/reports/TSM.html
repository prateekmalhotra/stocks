What the Market is Pricing In

At today's price of $418.95, Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing trades at elevated multiples of 37.1x P/OE and 35.8x EV/OE, implying sustained pricing power and near-monopolistic capture of leading-edge AI compute silicon demand. Mr. Market is pricing in flawless execution across the 2nm (N2) ramp and seamless CoWoS advanced packaging scaling, while largely looking past cyclicality in consumer end-markets.

However, skeptics highlight significant structural margin risks embedded in TSM's aggressive global geographic expansion. Guidance for FY2026 capital expenditures of $60B–$64B—alongside overseas mega-fab buildouts in Arizona, Kumamoto, and Dresden operating at 20% to 50% higher cost baselines—is expected to create an unprecedented wall of fixed depreciation expense. Wall Street bears argue that if hyperscaler infrastructure investments hit a digestion plateau, reverse operating leverage and non-Taiwan margin dilution of 300 to 400 basis points will severely compress corporate cash conversion.

Moderate Cyclical
Consumer retail, merchant payments, or advertising (e.g. StoneCo, Amazon, Lululemon). Earnings fluctuate with interest rates and consumer spending, but long-term secular growth continues.
Deep Cyclical
Commodities, homebuilders, freight shipping, or manufacturing (e.g. NVR, Vale, GigaCloud). Earnings swing between extreme peaks and valleys. Never extrapolate peak earnings.
