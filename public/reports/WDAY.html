01

What the Market is Pricing In

At today's market price of $200.01, Mr. Market is pricing Workday (WDAY) at a 40.4x P/OE multiple (39.3x EV/OE) and a meager 2.5% Owner Cash Yield. This valuation reflects significant market skepticism regarding the durability of enterprise software unit economics during an aggressive industry-wide shift toward AI-centric architectures. Recent sell-side downgrades from Wall Street firms—including Deutsche Bank, BTIG, and Morgan Stanley—highlight fears over decelerating subscription revenue growth (guided down to 12%–13% YoY for FY27) and a moderating non-GAAP operating margin trajectory capped around 30.0%.

Investors are increasingly concerned that the transition from per-seat enterprise HCM licensing to autonomous AI agent workflows (such as Workday Illuminate and Sana) will impair core seat retention as clients optimize headcount. Furthermore, Wall Street bears point to rising AI capital expenditures, low current monetization yields from early-stage AI add-ons, recent executive transitions, and intense competition from ServiceNow, Oracle, and SAP. At 40.4x normalized owner earnings, the current share price leaves zero margin of safety for execution stumbles.

How Things Are Going Now

Despite broader market apprehension and takeover speculation, Workday's operational core continues to demonstrate steady execution anchored by its mission-critical enterprise moat. The platform currently supports over 11,500 active enterprise customers—including more than 65% of the Fortune 500—and holds contract coverage for over 75 million individual users. In the latest quarter, Workday generated $2,542.0M in GAAP total revenue (up 13.5% YoY), establishing a real-time annualized run-rate of $10,168.0M. Backlog momentum remains steady: 12-month current subscription backlog (cRPO) reached $8,806M (+15.5% YoY), while total subscription backlog expanded to $27,294M.

Underlying software unit economics remain strong, characterized by a 76.2% gross margin and an audited Owner ROIC of 23.7%. The balance sheet maintains robust financial health with $4,353.0M in cash and marketable securities against $2,988.0M in total debt, yielding a net cash cushion of $1,365M ($+5.37/share). Management continues to balance organic platform investments—particularly in Financial Management (FINS), Workday Illuminate AI, and recent tuck-ins like Sana and Paradox—with disciplined shareholder capital returns.

While forward non-GAAP operating margin targets are re-anchored at ~30.0% and annual subscription growth is guided to 12%–13%, core gross retention remains stellar at 97%. Workday remains deeply entrenched as the central administrative system of record across global enterprise environments.
