01

What the Market is Pricing In

At the current stock price of $534.00, Northrop Grumman Corporation (NOC) trades at 21.7x P/OE and 25.4x EV/OE following a post-earnings rally driven by its Q3 2026 earnings release. Diluted EPS of $7.89 beat consensus expectations and full-year guidance was raised, but market participants remain skeptical regarding the underlying cash quality. A meaningful portion of the Q3 beat was driven by non-operational factors, specifically an unusually low effective tax rate of 13.4% and $371M in cash proceeds from selling a prototype B-21 test article back to the U.S. Air Force. Excluding these one-time items, operational cash generation was subdued, creating tension over whether current peak-multiple pricing is warranted.

The central market debate centers on whether Northrop Grumman's record backlog of $109.6B and entrenched sole-source positioning can outrun persistent fixed-price manufacturing liabilities and elevated capital requirements. Skeptics argue the equity is priced for perfection while still exposed to roughly $1.0B in unliquidated B-21 Raider low-rate initial production (LRIP) loss reserves, potential cost growth on the Sentinel ICBM program following its Nunn-McCurdy breach, and an upcoming $2.1B CapEx budget in 2027 that limits near-term free cash flow. In contrast, bulls believe the enterprise is at the beginning of an extended multi-year revenue ramp, where maturing serial production lots and steady defense procurement will unlock substantial cash flow compounding.
