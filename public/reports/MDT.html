01

What the Market is Pricing In

At the current market price of $93.35 (12.5x P/OE and 11.2x EV/OE), Mr. Market is pricing in severe execution drag and gross margin compression across Medtronic's medical device footprint. Investors are deeply concerned by a forecasted $250 million direct trade tariff hit to cost of goods sold in FY27, ongoing procedural headwinds in bariatric surgery caused by the rapid adoption of GLP-1 receptor agonists, and aggressive internal product cannibalization where rapid growth in Pulsed Field Ablation (PFA) is coming at the direct expense of high-margin legacy Cryoablation revenue (which collapsed 40% YoY).

Additionally, Wall Street is modeling terminal share loss in key growth vectors, noting that Medtronic's TAVR platform growth has stalled, while the Hugo Robotic-Assisted Surgery (RAS) platform continues to lag behind Intuitive Surgical's dominant da Vinci footprint. The market's valuation reflects a consensus belief that Medtronic's underlying organic growth remains stuck in the mid-single digits when stripped of extra calendar selling weeks, leaving little room for margin expansion amidst freight inflation and restructuring costs associated with the MiniMed separation.
