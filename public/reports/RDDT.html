What the Market is Pricing In

Mr. Market is currently pricing in persistent structural concerns around user acquisition channels and revenue sustainability at today's $153.29 stock price (19.2x P/OE, 17.9x EV/OE). Skeptics point to domestic daily active user stagnation, sequential US DAU softening, and high traffic reliance on Google organic search referrals. The planned retirement of logged-in versus logged-out metric disclosures from Q3 2026 onward is viewed as a red flag indicating slowing conversion of high-intent core users. Furthermore, data licensing revenue growth has decelerated to 24% YoY ($43.0M), shrinking to just 5.3% of total revenue and challenging the thesis that Reddit is an irreplaceable AI training data monopoly.

The market's core investment asymmetry centers on whether Reddit's margin expansion represents temporary ad-load optimization on a flattening user base, or the early stages of long-term commercial unit monetization. Skeptics fear that AI search engines (like ChatGPT and Perplexity) will increasingly disintermediate Reddit's user-generated content, while fixed infrastructure obligations—such as an $880.0M AWS multi-year cloud commitment—will burden future cash flows as revenue growth naturally slows.
