Section 1: Business Model, Scale Moat & GenAI Transition

Reddit, Inc. (NYSE: RDDT) operates one of the world's largest repository of human-curated context, topical discussions, and community consensus. As generative AI models proliferate across the internet with synthetic content, authentic human experience and real-time discourse have transformed into an increasingly scarce economic asset. Reddit monetizes this digital city through two primary engines: an expanding performance digital advertising engine (representing ~95% of total revenue) and high-margin AI data licensing agreements with frontier LLM developers like Google and OpenAI.

From a first-principles perspective, Reddit exhibits the quintessential characteristics of a capital-light economic moat: zero physical infrastructure inventory, user-generated content creation, self-moderating community governance structures, and extreme Operating Cash Flow conversion. The recent SEC Form 8-K filing on August 12, 2026 (Item 5.02) reported an orderly executive leadership transition in the legal department—Chief Legal Officer Benjamin Lee stepping down effective September 14, 2026, with veteran corporate legal chief Paul Cappuccio appointed to the role. Crucially, the filing confirmed zero disagreements between the departing officer and the board regarding operations, financial reporting, or corporate strategy.

Benjamin Graham & Warren Buffett Anti-Mr. Market Check: Mr. Market has knocked RDDT shares down to $153.45—a 20% decline from recent highs—conflating routine legal executive turnover and temporary Google search referral volatility with structural business impairment. Yet, evaluating Reddit under the 5-Year Market Closure Test reveals an enterprise generating $1.02B in trailing twelve-month operating cash flow, maintaining a $2.8B liquid cash fortress with zero interest-bearing debt, and compounding revenue at 61% YoY with 91.3% gross margins.
