01

What the Market is Pricing In

At the current market price of $155.29, Reddit trades at an aggressive multiple of 87.3x normalized Owner Earnings and 83.1x EV/OE. Mr. Market is pricing in sustained flawless execution, uninterrupted hyper-growth across international advertising tiers, and aggressive monetization from artificial intelligence data licensing agreements. Investors paying this premium assume that Reddit's transition from an under-monetized discussion board into an enterprise-grade digital ad powerhouse will continue without friction, expanding operating cash margins deep into mature megacap tech territory.

Beneath this exuberant multiple lies an active and well-founded skeptical counter-narrative, reflected in elevated short interest exceeding 16% of the public float. Bearish analysts argue that Reddit's traffic surge is structurally fragile and heavily reliant on search engine indexing algorithms that are increasingly vulnerable to Google's aggressive rollout of native AI Overviews. If AI-mediated search summaries eliminate outbound referral clicks to Reddit threads, top-of-funnel logged-out traffic could face sudden decay, exposing an inflated valuation.

How Things Are Going Now

Reddit's actual operational performance through fiscal year 2025 and entering early 2026 reflects powerful commercial momentum and operating leverage. Trailing twelve-month revenue for fiscal 2025 reached $2,202.5M, while the latest fourth quarter of 2025 generated $804.9M in revenue (up 61% year-over-year), establishing an annualized revenue run-rate of $3,219.6M heading into 2026. Gross margin performance remained stellar at 91.3%, driven by the capital-light nature of user-generated content and volunteer community moderation. Adjusted EBITDA margins expanded to 43% in the latest quarter of 2025, up from 40% in prior quarters, demonstrating strong operational flow-through.

Platform engagement units heading into 2026 continue to hit record highs across every metric. Daily Active Uniques (DAUq) climbed to 130.3 million to close out 2025, marking an 18% year-over-year expansion, while Weekly Active Uniques (WAUq) reached 514.6 million, up 24% year-over-year. Unit monetization is compounding rapidly into 2026, with global ARPU jumping 36% year-over-year to $6.18 (and U.S. ARPU reaching $11.85), propelled by higher ad pricing, conversion APIs, and Dynamic Product Ads. Reddit delivered $260.7M in quarterly free cash flow in late 2025, crossing $1.0B in trailing twelve-month operating cash flow.

Balance sheet management and capital allocation reflect sound discipline entering fiscal 2026. Reddit holds zero long-term debt against $1,487M in liquid cash and investments, equating to $7.36/share in net cash. Management authorized a $1.0B share repurchase program, retiring $235M of shares in the latest quarter to mitigate equity dilution as stock-based compensation declined 27% year-over-year in late 2025.

1. What the Market is Pricing In

Mr. Market is currently pricing Reddit at $153.53 per share, representing a premium valuation of 46.7x P/OE and 42.5x EV/OE. This pricing reflects immense optimism surrounding the company's recent consecutive quarters of over 60% year-over-year revenue growth, its high-margin AI data-licensing agreements, and its rapid expansion of adjusted EBITDA margins to over 42%. The market is treating Reddit as an indispensable, high-growth data monopoly that will continuously feed the hunger of large language models while scaling its advertising engine across a global user base.

However, this multiple leaves virtually no margin of safety, implying that the current hyper-growth phase is highly sustainable and immune to cyclical downturns. Skeptics argue that the market is dangerously anchoring to peak earnings and temporary data-licensing windfalls. They point to structural ceilings in high-value domestic user growth, the platform's fragile reliance on unpaid moderators, and the potential commoditization of LLM training data as critical risks that could trigger severe multiple compression.

Furthermore, the recent Q2 2026 earnings call highlighted a critical vulnerability: a decline in Google search referral traffic due to Google's AI-generated answers. This "choppy search referrals" warning from CEO Steve Huffman triggered a brutal 21% post-earnings selloff, exposing Reddit's fragile reliance on search engine indexing for top-of-funnel user acquisition. With US logged-in user growth flattening and the company guiding for a growth deceleration in Q3 2026 (47% to 49% YoY), the market is beginning to realize that Reddit's hyper-growth story has a structural ceiling.

2. Why the Market Might Be Right

The bear case for Reddit is not merely a valuation argument; it is a structural critique of its user acquisition and monetization engines. The core vulnerabilities center on U.S. DAU saturation, where growth has slowed to low-single digits, and the high-margin AI data-licensing segment, which faces renewal cliff risks as foundational LLM developers shift toward synthetic data or achieve data saturation. Additionally, aggressive ad-load increases risk alienating the core community, and any friction with unpaid moderators could disrupt safe ad inventory.

In a downside scenario, these headwinds materialize simultaneously, leading to a severe contraction in both traffic and monetization yield.

Secular Compounder

Moderate Cyclical
Consumer retail, merchant payments, or advertising (e.g. StoneCo, Amazon, Lululemon). Earnings fluctuate with interest rates and consumer spending, but long-term secular growth continues.
Deep Cyclical
Commodities, homebuilders, freight shipping, or manufacturing (e.g. NVR, Vale, GigaCloud). Earnings swing between extreme peaks and valleys. Never extrapolate peak earnings.

Downcycle Contraction / Trough
Operating under high interest rates, credit contractions, or inventory destocking. Starting earnings are depressed, offering asymmetric upside when conditions normalize.
Mid-Cycle Run-Rate
Operating near sustainable historical margins and normal unit economics. Reliable baseline for 3-year compounding calculations.
Peak / Over-Earning Risk
Generating temporary peak margins due to shortages or bubble pricing. Downside risk if margins mean-revert to historical levels.
