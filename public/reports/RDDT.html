01

What the Market is Pricing In

At $153.29 per share, Reddit trades at 45.6x audited normalized Owner Earnings (and 41.6x EV/OE with a 2.2% Owner Yield), reflecting intense debate between top-line hyper-growth (+61% YoY) and structural top-of-funnel operational decay. Major sell-side institutions—including Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, MoffettNathanson, and Bernstein—have re-evaluated price targets following earnings releases, flagging sequential softness in high-monetization U.S. daily active users (which drifted from 53.5 million to 53.2 million). Wall Street bears argue that Reddit's heavy dependency on Google Search indexing creates existential vulnerability as Google rolls out zero-click AI Overviews, which answer user queries directly on search engine results pages and intercept top-of-funnel logged-out traffic.

Simultaneously, the market heavily discounts Reddit’s rapid international user expansion (+28% YoY DAUs) due to severe monetization friction. International ARPU of $2.26 generates less than a fifth of U.S. ARPU ($11.85), raising concerns that global user growth will fail to offset domestic engagement saturation. Skeptics also question the long-term durability of high-margin AI data licensing agreements with OpenAI and Google, anticipating significant renewal pushback in 2026/2027 if generative AI models discontinue outbound link referrals. Compounded by heavy stock-based compensation dilution and ongoing executive stock liquidations, current pricing implies that Reddit's operational leverage could hit an external traffic bottleneck.

How Things Are Going Now

Despite search algorithm anxieties, Reddit's fundamental operating engine continues to demonstrate high velocity. In the latest reported quarter, revenue expanded 61% YoY to $804.9 million, driving a record annualized run-rate of $3,219.6 million. Gross margins reached 91.3%, verifying the capital-light nature of community-moderated, user-generated content hosted on scalable cloud infrastructure. User volume metrics remain robust, with global Daily Active Uniques (DAUq) reaching 130.3 million (+18% YoY) and global Weekly Active Uniques (WAUq) reaching 514.6 million (+24% YoY).

Monetization efficiency is advancing globally. Global ARPU rose 36% YoY to $6.18, anchored by a 51% surge in U.S. ARPU to $11.85 and a 31% gain in International ARPU to $2.26. Performance advertising products like Reddit Max and Conversion APIs (CAPI) are accelerating lower-funnel conversion rates and expanding advertiser adoption. Meanwhile, non-advertising revenue—led by enterprise AI data licensing deals—contributed $43 million in the quarter at near 100% incremental gross margins.

Reddit maintains a fortress balance sheet with $2,790.0 million in cash and liquid investments against $20.9 million in total debt, establishing a net cash position of $+13.44 per share ($2,769 million total). Capital expenditures were kept to under 1% of revenue ($7.2 million maintenance CapEx), allowing operating cash flows to convert cleanly into free cash flow. While sequential U.S. DAU movement exhibited minor volatility (53.2 million vs. 53.5 million prior quarter), unit monetization expansion continues to drive overall top- and bottom-line growth.
