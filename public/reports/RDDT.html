1. Core Economic Engine & Revenue Growth Breakdown

Reddit, Inc. (NYSE: RDDT) operates as an internet forum platform organized around community-driven discussions across specialized subreddits. In an online environment increasingly saturated with synthetic and automated AI-generated content, Reddit’s core value proposition rests on providing unfiltered, authentic human conversation and contextual intent. In Q2 2026, Reddit generated $805 million in total revenue, marking a 61% YoY increase and its eighth consecutive quarter of 60%+ top-line growth. Adjusted EBITDA reached $343 million (a 43% margin, up 106% YoY), net income surged to $253 million (a 31% net margin), and quarterly free cash flow hit $261 million (+135% YoY).

Advertising Revenue ($762 Million, +64% YoY)

Advertising forms the vast majority (94.7%) of Reddit’s revenue engine. Growth during the quarter was fueled by a combination of strong ad pricing realization, impression expansion, and accelerated customer acquisition across mid-market and performance-focused channels:

  • Ad Pricing & Auction Mechanics: Effective ad pricing grew approximately 40% YoY, demonstrating expanding pricing power as advertisers place higher monetary value on high-intent user communities. Ad impressions simultaneously grew 17% YoY.
  • Advertiser Base Scaling: Active advertisers increased by more than 70% YoY, propelled by the adoption of automated campaign management tools. Scaled channel revenues doubled year-over-year as small and mid-sized businesses (SMBs) utilized self-service ad toolkits.
  • Performance & Commerce Ad Formats: Product releases such as Reddit Max (automated campaign optimization), Dynamic Product Ads (DPA), and deep platform integration with Shopify have moved Reddit down the marketing funnel from brand awareness to direct-response commerce conversion.

Data Licensing & Other Revenue ($43 Million, +24% YoY)

Data Licensing and non-advertising revenue totaled $43 million, representing 5.3% of total revenue. While small relative to digital advertising, this higher-margin stream captures the strategic value of Reddit's structured conversational data. Major AI developers pay recurring licensing fees to access Reddit's Data API for real-time model training, post-training alignment, and RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) search grounding.

Section 5: Intrinsic Valuation & Warren Buffett Owner Earnings Analysis

To establish a realistic, level-headed valuation of Reddit, Inc. (RDDT), we apply Warren Buffett’s intrinsic value framework as articulated throughout his Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letters. Rather than relying on arbitrary exit multiples—which Charlie Munger famously dismissed as assuming a "greater fool" in Year 5—we value the business strictly on the present value of its future net cash flows, treating Stock-Based Compensation (SBC) as an inescapable cash-equivalent cost and strictly isolating maintenance capital expenditures from discretionary expansion.

1. Derivation of Normalized Owner Earnings

Buffett’s 1986 letter defines Owner Earnings as Operating Cash Flow minus Maintenance CapEx, adjusted for real dilution. In Reddit’s high-margin, capital-light digital architecture, physical CapEx requirements are negligible (~0.27% of total revenue). However, stock-based compensation represents a major structural drag on existing shareholders and must be deducted in full.

Section 6: Thesis Confidence, Execution Risk & Pre-Mortem Framework

While Reddit’s unit economics reflect world-class operational leverage (crossing $1 million revenue per employee with a Rule of 40 score exceeding 100%), realizing our $185.00 Base Case target requires careful monitoring of structural execution risks and algorithmic headwinds.

1. Execution Risk Assessment

  • Search Engine Referral Volatility: A significant portion of Reddit’s top-of-funnel logged-out traffic relies on Google search algorithm indexing. Changes to Google’s SGE (Search Generative Experience) or core updates introduce traffic choppiness, creating volatility in total Daily Active Uniques (DAUq).
  • US User Stagnation Risk: While overall DAUq expanded 18% YoY to 130.3 million driven by international adoption, domestic US DAUq stagnated sequentially in recent quarters. Because US users generate higher Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) than international users, re-accelerating US engagement is vital to sustain ad yield expansion.
  • AI Data Licensing Renewal Friction: Data licensing agreements with OpenAI and Google represent high-margin non-ad revenue (~$60M–$65M annually). As contracts approach renewal, any partner pushback or pricing compression poses a potential headwind to incremental operating margins.
Pre-Mortem Falsification Triggers (Automatic Thesis Invalidation):

We will immediately lower our intrinsic valuation estimate or exit the position if any of the following three falsification triggers occur:

  1. US Engagement Contraction: US Daily Active Uniques (DAUq) contract by more than 5.0% year-over-year for two consecutive quarters, signaling core saturation or algorithmic displacement.
  2. SBC Expense Erosion: Stock-based compensation re-accelerates above 35% of revenue, eroding post-SBC Owner Earnings margins below 20.0%.
  3. AI Licensing Impairment: Tier-1 AI data licensing renewals occur at haircuts greater than 25.0% relative to initial contract run-rates, or key partners fail to renew.
