Section 1: The Premise of the Company

1.1 Executive Summary & Business Model Architecture

Reddit, Inc. (NYSE: RDDT) operates one of the world's most authentic, intent-rich conversational platforms, comprising over 100,000 active subreddits and 130.3 million daily active unique users (DAUq). The enterprise monetizes primarily via performance advertising (direct-response and brand display), contextual search advertising, and enterprise data licensing partnerships.

The Core Investment Thesis: Reddit is transitioning from an under-monetized social forum into a high-intent, machine-learning-powered advertising and commercial search platform. In Q2 2026, revenue accelerated +61% YoY to $805M, with GAAP operating margins reaching 28.8% and Adjusted EBITDA margins hitting 42.6%. Crucially, this growth was driven by a +40% increase in ad pricing (eCPM) alongside only +17% ad volume growth, proving that monetization is scaling through conversion effectiveness rather than feed degradation. While the headline multiple appears elevated ($154.96 against $1.65/share trailing Normalized Owner Earnings), a disciplined 5-year bridge shows that Reddit has a credible path to ~$9.0B in revenue and ~$2.7B in Normalized Owner Earnings (~30% OE margin). Discounted at a strict 9.5% hurdle rate, intrinsic fair value evaluates to $158.32 / share, placing the stock roughly at fair value and warranting a HOLD stance with high-conviction accumulation below $120.00.

1.2 The Real Growth Engine: Ad Pricing (+40%) vs. Ad Volume (+17%)

The defining insight into Reddit's monetization quality is the structural decoupling of pricing and ad load:

  • High-Quality Monetization: Q2 2026 ad impressions grew ~17% YoY, while ad prices (eCPM / conversion value) surged ~40% YoY. Revenue expansion is not coming from ad-stuffing user feeds, but from higher advertiser density, CAPI conversion tracking, and direct-response targeting.
  • Massive International Runway: U.S. ARPU reached $11.85 (+51% YoY), while International ARPU stood at $2.26 (+31% YoY). U.S. monetization is 5.2x International monetization. Even converging international ARPU to half of U.S. levels ($5.93) represents a +162% international revenue expansion on an existing 130M+ user base.

1.3 AI Licensing: Treated Strictly as Unfunded Call Optionality

While Reddit's multi-year licensing deals with Google and OpenAI generate high-margin cash flows, advertising generates ~95% of total revenue ($762M out of $805M in Q2 2026). We treat AI data licensing strictly as an unfunded call option rather than a mandatory pillar of the thesis. If AI licensing expands, it provides pure incremental upside; if licensing yields compress, the core advertising and search business is more than sufficient to underwrite the valuation.

1.4 Moat Analysis: Narrow Moat with Expanding Search Network Effects

Reddit's economic moat is classified as Narrow Moat, anchored by four structural advantages:

  1. 20-Year Searchable Intent Archive: Over two decades of human discussions across 100,000+ communities create an organic SEO moat that captures millions of high-intent Google searches (e.g. queries ending in '...reddit').
  2. Distributed Subreddit Moderation Structure: Unpaid, passionate community moderators curate millions of niche topics, providing quality control and brand safety without requiring immense corporate moderation overhead.
  3. High-Intent Purchase Consideration: Unlike passive entertainment feeds (TikTok, Instagram Reels), users visit Reddit actively seeking product advice, technical recommendations, and honest consumer reviews.

Section 2: The Probable Future Paths

Path 1: International ARPU Convergence & Contextual Ad Scaling (50% Weight - Base Case)

Operational Summary: Reddit scales top-line revenue at a +32.5% CAGR to $9.0B over 5 years (from $2.2B FY25, or ~22% CAGR from TTM run-rate). Growth is driven by international ARPU converging toward $5.00+, machine-learning search ads, and direct-response advertiser density (+40% ad pricing leverage). Operating margins expand to 38.0% and SBC moderates to 8% of revenue, producing a 30.0% Normalized Owner Earnings margin ($2.70B total OE, or $13.00 / share). Applying a mature 20.0x terminal multiple plus $10.00/share net cash yields a 5-year target price of $270.00 (Present Fair Value: $171.51 at 9.5% hurdle).

Revenue reaches $5.0B (+17.8% CAGR). High persistent SBC and moderation overhead hold Owner Earnings margins at 20.0% ($1.0B total OE, or $4.80 / share). Applying a compressed 16.0x terminal multiple plus $8.00 net cash yields a 5-year target price of $84.80 (Present Fair Value: $53.87, representing a -65% drawdown vs. $154.96).

Applying a 22.5x terminal multiple plus $12.00 net cash yields a 5-year target price of $444.00 (Present Fair Value: $282.04).

Market Inversion & Valuation Synthesis

Disaggregating the Reverse-DCF: At today's market price of $154.96, Mr. Market values Reddit at 93.9x Normalized Owner Earnings ($1.65/share) (or 86.6x Ex-Cash after deducting $12.03/share in cash). It is vital to understand the two distinct questions answered by the reverse-DCF:

  • 1. Multiple Persistence Benchmark (93.9x P/OE₀): If one assumes the market's 93.9x multiple remains permanently frozen for 5 years, Reddit only needs to grow Owner Earnings at +8.4% / yr to achieve the 9.5% hurdle rate. This illustrates pure multiple dependency.
  • 2. Multiple Normalization Benchmark (20.0x P/OE₅ - The Graham-Buffett Standard): In reality, as Reddit matures from hyper-growth to a stable advertising titan, its multiple will compress toward 20.0x P/OE. Under this realistic normalization, Reddit must organically compound per-share Owner Earnings at +32.6% / yr over 5 years (from $1.65 to $6.75/share) to achieve the 9.5% annual hurdle rate.
