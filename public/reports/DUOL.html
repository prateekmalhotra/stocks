Section 1: The Premise of the Company

1. The Core Business Machine, Moat & Unit Economics

Duolingo, Inc. (NASDAQ: DUOL) operates the world's leading mobile language learning platform, leveraging a highly gamified, freemium consumer model that has successfully democratized education while building an exceptional cash-generating enterprise. The company's core economic engine is driven by organic user acquisition, proprietary pedagogical algorithms, and daily habit loop mechanics (e.g., streak rewards, leaderboards, and personalized notifications) that convert passive learners into highly retained, paying subscribers. As of the second quarter of fiscal year 2026 (period ended June 30, 2026), Duolingo reached an unprecedented scale of 58.7 million Daily Active Users (DAUs, up 23% year-over-year) and 12.7 million paid subscribers (up 17% year-over-year).

Primary Economic Moat Rating: Narrow Moat. Duolingo possesses a durable economic moat anchored in high customer switching costs derived from structural psychological lock-in (multi-year learning streaks and accrued user progress) and powerful network effects driven by viral social mechanics and word-of-mouth distribution. While consumer software generally faces low friction to churn, Duolingo's product design creates a distinct "habit flywheel" where over 5 million daily learners hold active streaks exceeding one year. This organic user acquisition engine enables Duolingo to spend under 15% of revenues on sales and marketing—an extraordinarily low acquisition cost profile compared to traditional consumer software platforms.

The core monetization architecture spans five distinct revenue streams:

  • Paid Subscriptions (Super Duolingo & Duolingo Max): Represents 86.2% of total revenue. Offers an ad-free experience, unlimited hearts, progress tracking, and AI-powered interactive tools such as real-time Video Call conversations with the AI persona Lily.
  • Advertising: Represents 7.1% of total revenue. Monetizes the non-paying free user base (~91% of Monthly Active Users) via display and video ads delivered at the conclusion of lessons.
  • Duolingo English Test (DET): Represents 3.9% of total revenue. An AI-proctored, on-demand digital English proficiency assessment accepted by over 6,100 higher education programs worldwide as a modern alternative to TOEFL/IELTS.
  • In-App Purchases (IAPs): Represents 2.9% of total revenue. Transactional sales of virtual items, streak freezes, and gem packs.
  • Other Revenue: Represents 0.2% of total revenue. Includes gear, licensing, and miscellaneous corporate initiatives.

Buffett & Munger Pricing Power & Inflation Resistance Audit

Pricing Power Classification: Strong Pricing Power. Duolingo exhibits significant unilateral pricing discretion driven by its high consumer value surplus and low absolute dollar cost.

The 'Prayer Session' Test: Management does not require a "prayer session" before adjusting subscription price points or restructuring feature tiers. In 2023, the company seamlessly launched Duolingo Max at a premium price point ($168/year or $29.99/month) to capture surplus value from advanced learners seeking generative AI speaking practice. In 2026, management strategic initiatives expanded Video Call functionality down into the core Super Duolingo tier to widen the top-of-funnel conversion funnel, demonstrating complete control over product bundling and price realization.

Customer Share of Wallet: An annual subscription to Super Duolingo (~$80 to $120/year, or ~$6.99 to $9.99/month) represents a trivial fraction of a consumer's annual discretionary entertainment or self-improvement budget—especially when evaluated against formal private tutoring ($30–$60 per hour) or traditional language institutes ($1,000+ per course).

Buffett & Munger Cash Flow Predictability & "Too Hard" Pile Audit

Predictability Classification: Moderate Predictability.

The 10-Year Visibility Test: While language learning is an enduring human necessity that does not suffer from fashion obsolescence, consumer software applications face ongoing technology shifts. The rapid evolution of standalone real-time AI translation devices and general LLMs requires Duolingo to continuously innovate its gamified interface to retain user attention. However, because approximately 86% of revenues are collected upfront via annual auto-renewing subscriptions, intermediate term visibility (12 to 36 months) is exceptionally strong, as evidenced by $505.1 million in deferred revenue liabilities on the balance sheet as of Q2 2026.

Obsolescence & Reinvestment Drag: Duolingo avoids the "Red Queen" trap of heavy capital reinvestment. Rather than consuming cash to replace hardware, the company leverages internal generative AI tools to accelerate content creation. In Q1 2026, Duolingo published 20,500 new course skills compared to 1,800 skills per quarter in 2024, driving dramatic operating leverage across R&D.

Margin of Safety Compensator: A fortress balance sheet holding over $1.31 billion in cash and zero funded debt provides an absolute downside safety buffer during major operational pivots or technology transition phases.

Section 2: 3 Probable Business Stories

Based on the company's core premise, reported operational metrics, financial filings, and 4-quarter earnings trajectory, here are 3 distinct, probable fundamental paths that cover 90%–95% of probable business outcomes over the next 3–5 years:

📖 Story 1: Re-Accelerated Freemium Flywheel & Super Tier AI Expansion (Central Baseline)

Under this baseline path, Duolingo's strategic pivot executed in early 2026—deliberately lowering friction, expanding generative AI capabilities like real-time Video Call with Lily down into the core Super Duolingo tier, and prioritizing long-term top-of-funnel engagement—successfully reinvigorates user growth. The company avoids over-monetizing its free tier, preserving its viral, low-cost organic customer acquisition flywheel (Sales & Marketing remains below 14% of revenues). Daily Active Users (DAUs) expand from 58.7 million in Q2 2026 to approximately 95.0 million by FY 2029 (a ~13% CAGR), driven by growth in under-penetrated international markets across Latin America, Southeast Asia, and Eastern Europe.

Paid subscriber conversion stabilizes at roughly 8.5% of Monthly Active Users (MAUs), as users convert to Super Duolingo for ad-free learning and integrated AI speaking practice. The premium Duolingo Max tier caters to a loyal core of advanced language learners willing to pay $168/year, representing 15% of the total paid subscriber base. The Duolingo English Test (DET) continues its steady institutional adoption, expanding from 6,100 to over 8,500 higher education programs, while non-language subjects (Math and Music) provide incremental retention benefits without incurring heavy dedicated marketing costs.

Operational Metric Drivers & Revenue:

  • DAUs / MAUs: DAUs grow from 58.7M to 95.0M by FY 2029; MAUs grow from ~115M to 185M.
  • Paid Subscriber Count: Paid subscribers expand from 12.7M to 15.7M by FY 2029 (8.5% conversion rate on MAUs).
  • Top-Line Revenue Trajectory: Subscription revenues grow at a 15% CAGR from $873.4M in FY25 to $1,525M in FY29. Advertising, DET, and IAP revenues compound at 10% annually to reach $245M. Total Net Revenue reaches approximately $1.77 Billion USD in FY 2029 (a ~14.3% overall 4-year CAGR).

Cost Dynamics, CapEx & Owner Earnings: Gross margins remain resilient at 71.5%–72.5%, as internal generative AI authoring tools offset third-party cloud compute costs for Video Call features. Operating expenses scale moderately; R&D headcount growth slows as AI tools handle repetitive content generation (publishing over 30,000 course skills per quarter). Stock-Based Compensation (SBC) as a percentage of revenue declines from 13.2% in FY25 to ~9.0% ($160M USD) as stock award dilution is offset by ongoing share buybacks funded from operational cash flows. Maintenance CapEx remains modest at $25M annually. Under this trajectory, GAAP Operating Cash Flow scales to $580M USD by FY 2029. Deducting $25M in Maintenance CapEx, $160M in SBC, and $45M in non-operating treasury interest yield normalization yields a Core Operating Baseline Owner Earnings (OE) of $350 Million USD by FY 2029.

Key Milestones to Watch: Maintenance of DAU YoY growth above 18%; paid subscriber penetration holding above 8.2% of MAUs; SBC expense trending below 10% of total net revenue.

📖 Story 2: Duolingo Max Hyper-Adoption & Global Multimodal Scaling (Upside Trajectory)

In this operational trajectory, Duolingo's investments in proprietary AI pedagogy create an undeniable competitive moat that transforms the platform from a language tool into an irreplaceable global learning tutor across multiple disciplines. Real-time AI Video Calls, dynamic personalized lesson generation, and real-world conversational roleplay drive massive consumer enthusiasm. The Duolingo Max tier ($29.99/month or $168/year) experiences hyper-adoption, scaling from under 10% of total subscribers in 2025 to over 30% by FY 2029. Advanced learners, corporate enterprise clients, and parents buying multi-user Family Plans view Duolingo as a dramatically superior, cost-effective substitute for $50/hour human private tutoring.

Furthermore, viral social mechanics and expanding content in Math, Music, and advanced literacy drive unprecedented user retention, pushing active learning streaks to historical highs. DAU growth remains elevated at 20%+ annually, taking DAUs to 125.0 million by FY 2029. Paid subscriber conversion rises from 8.6% to 11.5% of MAUs. Concurrently, the Duolingo English Test (DET) wins major government and university mandates in India, China, and Nigeria, becoming the global standard for international student and visa proficiency assessments, generating over $120M in high-margin testing revenues.

Operational Metric Drivers & Revenue:

  • DAUs / MAUs: DAUs scale from 58.7M to 125.0M by FY 2029 (~21% CAGR); MAUs reach 240M.
  • Paid Subscriber Count & Mix: Paid subscribers reach 27.6M (11.5% conversion of MAUs), with 8.3M subscribers on the higher-ARPU Duolingo Max tier. Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) expands significantly.
  • Top-Line Revenue Trajectory: Subscription revenues compound at 24% annually to $2.06 Billion USD by FY 2029. DET, Ads, and IAP generate $340M USD. Total Net Revenue expands to $2.40 Billion USD by FY 2029 (~23.3% 4-year CAGR).

Cost Dynamics, CapEx & Owner Earnings: Significant operating leverage takes GAAP operating margins to 25%. Gross margins expand slightly to 74.0% as higher-priced Max subscriptions comfortably absorb AI compute infrastructure charges and 15%–30% mobile app store commission structures. SBC drops to 7.0% of revenues ($168M USD) as stock grants moderate relative to massive revenue growth. Maintenance CapEx remains low at $30M USD. GAAP Operating Cash Flow surges to $850M USD. Subtracting $30M Maintenance CapEx, $168M SBC, and $52M treasury interest adjustments produces a Core Operating Baseline Owner Earnings (OE) of $600 Million USD by FY 2029.

Key Milestones to Watch: Duolingo Max reaching >25% of total paid subscriber mix; overall paid conversion breaching 10.0% of MAUs; DET annual revenue exceeding $80M.

📖 Story 3: Commoditized AI Voice Competition & Compute Margin Compression (Downside Trajectory)

This downside path models structural friction arising from rapid advancements in native, standalone consumer Generative AI tools. Free, real-time voice features embedded directly into mobile operating systems and general LLM platforms (such as OpenAI's Advanced Voice Mode, Gemini Live, and native Apple Intelligence tools) enable consumers to conduct free, highly fluent, multi-lingual voice conversations without requiring a dedicated language app subscription. This dilutes Duolingo's pricing power for its premium Duolingo Max tier, forcing management to bundle higher-cost AI features into lower tiers or keep prices flat to prevent user churn.

While Duolingo's gamification and streak mechanics protect its core causal user base, top-of-funnel growth slows as casual learners rely on ubiquitous AI tools. DAU growth decelerates to single-digits (~6% CAGR), reaching 72.0 million by FY 2029. Paid subscriber conversion ticks down from 8.6% to 7.0% of MAUs due to free alternative availability. Simultaneously, gross margins experience structural pressure from two fronts: mandatory 15%–30% App Store fees on iOS/Android, and high per-minute compute costs paid to external cloud/LLM providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, AWS) to power real-time Video Call latency, which cannot be passed fully to consumers.

Operational Metric Drivers & Revenue:

  • DAUs / MAUs: DAU growth slows to 6% annually, reaching 72.0M by FY 2029; MAUs plateau around 140M.
  • Paid Subscriber Count: Paid subscribers stagnate at 9.8M by FY 2029 (7.0% conversion rate on MAUs).
  • Top-Line Revenue Trajectory: Subscription revenues grow at just 5% annually to $1.06 Billion USD by FY 2029. Advertising and DET revenues remain flat at $120M total. Total Net Revenue reaches $1.18 Billion USD by FY 2029 (~3.2% CAGR from FY25).

Cost Dynamics, CapEx & Owner Earnings: Gross margins compress from 73.0% down to 66.0% due to un-recouped LLM compute costs and fixed platform fees. Operating leverage reverses; management is forced to maintain high sales and marketing spending ($220M USD) to retain users against competitive AI apps. SBC remains sticky at $150M USD annually to retain senior engineering talent. Maintenance CapEx rises to $35M USD to support infrastructure modernization. GAAP Operating Cash Flow drops to $280M USD. After subtracting $35M Maintenance CapEx, $150M SBC, and $35M in interest income normalization, Core Operating Baseline Owner Earnings (OE) shrink to $60 Million USD by FY 2029.

Key Milestones to Watch: Gross margins dropping below 69.0%; DAU YoY growth slowing to under 8%; paid subscriber count posting sequential quarterly declines; SBC exceeding 15% of total revenue.

  • Maintenance vs. Growth CapEx & AI Hardware Obsolescence: While consensus models assume nearly all CapEx drives growth, our framework accounts for ongoing infrastructure upkeep, server migration, and capitalized software amortization (~$20.0M LTM) as mandatory Maintenance CapEx required to retain existing daily active users.
