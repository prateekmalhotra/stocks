Section 1: The Premise of the Company

Joint Stock Company Kaspi.kz (NASDAQ: KSPI) operates an ultra-dominant, multi-sided digital ecosystem in Central Asia and is aggressively expanding into Türkiye. Structured around its consumer Super App and merchant Kaspi Pay platform, the company seamlessly integrates three high-frequency operating pillars: Marketplace (e-commerce, commercial catalog, travel, and logistics), Payments (P2P transfers, QR payments, bill payments, and merchant acquiring), and Fintech (BNPL, merchant trade finance, consumer loans, and deposits). By embedding itself into the daily economic fabric of Kazakhstan—where its consumer app reaches 15.7 million Monthly Active Users (MAU) out of an adult population of ~12 million, with an industry-leading Daily Active User (DAU) to MAU ratio of 68%—Kaspi.kz functions as a national economic tollbooth and digital highway.

Core Business Model, Unit Monetization & Growth Engine

Kaspi’s flywheel relies on extreme transaction frequency and zero-friction cross-platform monetization:

  • Payments Platform: Monetized via merchant processing fees (taking ~1.00%–1.07% on Total Payment Volume, or TPV) and interest earned on float held within term deposits ($80M+ annually). Payments serve as the low-friction acquisition funnel, driving consumer engagement to 77 transactions per active user per month.
  • Marketplace Platform: Monetized through seller take rates averaging 12.1% (expanding +110 bps YoY driven by high-margin value-added services like Kaspi Advertising and Kaspi Delivery) on Gross Merchandise Value (GMV). In 2025, Kaspi acquired an 85.17% controlling stake in Hepsiburada (D-MARKET Electronic Services), extending its marketplace model into Türkiye’s 85+ million population market.
  • Fintech Platform: Monetized through net interest margin (NIM) and fee revenue generated on Total Finance Volume (TFV), predominantly driven by short-duration (average maturity <6 months), high-velocity BNPL (Kaspi Red) and merchant working capital loans. Loan origination is funded by sticky, low-cost retail deposits held inside the Kaspi Super App.

Trailing 4-Quarter Earnings & Operational Commentary

Across the last four reported quarters (Q3 2025 through Q2 2026), Kaspi.kz demonstrated strong top-line compounding alongside disciplined balance sheet management, while navigating the near-term dilution of its Turkish expansion:

  • Q3 2025: Delivered KZT 1.12 trillion ($2.22B USD) in total revenue (+51% YoY), driven by the initial consolidation of Hepsiburada and a 51% surge in quarterly interest income. Net profit reached KZT 270B ($534M USD), as elevated base rates increased bank deposit funding costs.
  • Q4 2025: Concluded FY2025 with full-year revenue reaching $8,004M USD (+59.8% YoY) and net profit holding firm at $2,112M USD (+1.0% YoY). Marketplace GMV growth accelerated to +28% YoY on a constant currency basis, while Hepsiburada approached operational EBITDA breakeven ahead of management guidance.
  • Q1 2026: Revenue rose 31% YoY to KZT 1.08 trillion ($2.26B USD), led by Marketplace revenue of $1.08B USD (+58% YoY). Adjusted EBITDA expanded to $769M USD (+9% YoY). The company secured regulatory approval for its acquisition of Rabobank A.Ş. in Türkiye, laying the infrastructure for launching Kaspi Fintech products in Türkiye by 2027.
  • Q2 2026: Reported consolidated revenue of $2.30B USD (+15% YoY) and Adjusted EBITDA of $826M USD (+5% YoY). E-commerce GMV surged 28% YoY on a constant currency basis with annual purchases per consumer expanding to 15.8x. Management unveiled Kasper, an integrated AI shopping assistant, and recommended an 18% QoQ dividend step-up to KZT 1,000 per ADS.

Governance, Executive Control & C-Suite Audit

Kaspi.kz maintains high insider alignment and key-person stability. Co-Founder and CEO Mikheil Lomtadze and Non-Executive Chairman Vyacheslav Kim remain the primary strategic drivers, collectively controlling over 40% of voting equity. In early 2026, global technology conglomerate Tencent acquired a strategic equity stake alongside CEO Mikheil Lomtadze, validating Kaspi's Super App architecture.

Activist & Short-Seller Audit: In September 2024, short-seller Culper Research published a report alleging sanctions evasion and non-operating revenue inflation. Kaspi’s board and independent audit committee conducted an immediate review, issuing detailed factual rebuttals. Independent auditor Deloitte LLP issued an unmodified, clean audit opinion on Kaspi’s FY2025 Form 20-F (filed March 16, 2026), affirming internal financial controls and regulatory compliance.

Economic Moat Classification: Wide Moat

Kaspi.kz commands an insurmountable Wide Moat derived from structural multi-sided network effects, extreme ecosystem lock-in, and infrastructure scale density:

  • Multi-Sided Network Effects: 15.7 million consumers and 764,000 active merchants create an entrenched virtuous cycle. Merchant coverage is so dense that Kaspi Pay is the default national transaction standard in Kazakhstan.
  • High Switching Costs: Consumers store primary savings, receive salaries, pay bills, buy retail products, and book travel exclusively inside the Super App. Merchants rely on Kaspi for point-of-sale hardware, working capital credit, inventory listing, and last-mile delivery.
  • Cost Advantage & Tollbooth Status: Proprietary closed-loop payment rails bypass international card networks, driving customer acquisition costs (CAC) near zero and enabling mid-30s net profit margins.
