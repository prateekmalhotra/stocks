What the Market is Pricing In

Mr. Market is currently treating Intuit with extreme caution near $278.07, pricing the equity at a compressed 10.3x P/OE. The prevailing narrative assumes that Intuit's double-digit top-line momentum has been largely manufactured through aggressive ARPU price hikes rather than real customer volume expansion, pointing to softening online ecosystem net adds and lower-end DIY tax headwinds from free government tax filing alternatives.

Furthermore, the market views recent workforce restructurings (cutting 17% of roles while absorbing severance and facility realignment charges) as a defensive scramble to offset soaring generative AI compute costs and human-expert support overhead. Instead of valuing Intuit as an elite cash-generative compounder, Mr. Market prices in terminal growth deceleration and margin compression.

How Things Are Going Now

Despite market anxiety, Intuit's operational performance demonstrates exceptional underlying resilience. Full-year FY26 revenue reached $21,400.0M (+13.9% YoY), with the latest fourth quarter delivering $4,350.0M at an elite 78.2% gross margin. The business maintains a massive global footprint of 100 million total customers worldwide across Global Business Solutions and Consumer ecosystems (including QuickBooks, TurboTax, Credit Karma, and Mailchimp).

Growth is increasingly anchored by Intuit's 'Big Bets'—Assisted Tax (TurboTax Live), Money, and the Mid-Market Intuit Enterprise Suite (IES)—which grew north of 30% YoY and now comprise a vital portion of total revenue. On the capital allocation front, Intuit generates robust free cash flow, maintaining a clean balance sheet with $3,950.0M in liquid cash against $2,900.0M in total debt ($+1,050M net cash).

Management's strategic shift toward an AI-driven expert platform is expanding monetization beyond basic software subscriptions into transactional take-rates across merchant payment processing, payroll services, and liability-backed expert tax preparation. The mission-critical nature of SMB accounting systems of record creates exceptionally high switching costs and high retention rates across the core ecosystem.
