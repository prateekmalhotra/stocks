01

What the Market is Pricing In

At the current market price of $301.45, representing a valuation of 27.2x P/OE and 28.2x EV/OE, Mr. Market is pricing in severe skepticism regarding Intuit's long-term terminal value and competitive positioning. Wall Street's primary anxiety centers on artificial intelligence acting as a commoditizing force against Intuit's legacy franchises, most notably the TurboTax DIY ecosystem and entry-level QuickBooks workflows. Recent analyst commentary highlights deep concerns over a decelerating customer acquisition runway, where total online paying customer growth slowed to just 3% in fiscal 2026. Skeptics argue that lower-cost automated tax filers and generative AI accounting agents will dismantle the consumer tax moat and force pricing concessions across the board.

This dynamic creates an acute asymmetry in market expectations. While Intuit has historically commanded multiples exceeding 35x to 40x P/OE, the market is now aggressively discounting forward guidance—specifically the modest 9% overall revenue growth forecast and an anemic 2% projection for the Consumer Tax segment in fiscal 2027. Investors are wrestling with whether Intuit's current earnings power represents an unsustainable cyclical peak driven by legacy pricing power before AI disruption takes hold, or if the market is overly pessimistic about a wide-moat enterprise successfully migrating users toward higher-value assisted solutions like TurboTax Live and mid-market enterprise software.

Deep Cyclical
Commodities, homebuilders, freight shipping, or manufacturing (e.g. NVR, Vale, GigaCloud). Earnings swing between extreme peaks and valleys. Never extrapolate peak earnings.
