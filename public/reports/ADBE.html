Adobe (ADBE) Investment Thesis

The thesis demonstrates that generative AI is an enterprise tailwind rather than an existential threat because professional creators and corporations require legal indemnification, proprietary file formats (.ai, .psd, .pdf), and integrated creative pipelines. Unit economics indicate a new entrant would need to spend over $30 billion across five years in cumulative R&D and S&M to build a comparable enterprise suite. Valuation math yields a base-case intrinsic value of $431.97 per share (~45% upside) underpinned by 35%+ operating margins, high capital efficiency, and aggressive share repurchases.

Adobe ($ADBE): The Market Is Missing The Bigger Picture

The write-up analyzes Adobe's Q2 FY2026 earnings ($6.62 billion revenue, up 13% YoY) to show that Firefly AI features are enhancing retention and driving top-line acceleration rather than cannibalizing seat counts. With an ecosystem of over 850 million monthly active users, Adobe possesses an insurmountable distribution moat that allows it to seamlessly monetize AI tools across its existing subscriber base. High workflow switching costs across Creative Cloud and Digital Experience, paired with massive cash generation funding stock buybacks, support a strong long-term compounding narrative.
