01

What the Market is Pricing In

At today's market price of $275.30, representing valuation multiples of 34.4x P/OE and 33.2x EV/OE, Mr. Market is pricing in severe long-term fears of generative AI disintermediation and terminal moat degradation. The prevailing market consensus worries that standalone generative AI tools like Midjourney, OpenAI Sora, and Stable Diffusion are bypassing Adobe's complex creative application layer altogether. Furthermore, aggressive competition from Canva and Affinity at the prosumer low end, alongside Figma's continued dominance in web-native UI/UX design post-deal collapse, is seen as capping Creative Cloud's monetizable addressable market and triggering enterprise seat downgrades.

This pricing structure reflects deep skepticism regarding management's recent strategic pivot. The decision to pause scheduled Creative Cloud price increases for H2 FY26 and shift aggressively into freemium Firefly and Express onboarding is viewed not as a deliberate customer acquisition strategy, but as surrender of legacy pricing power that introduces a ~$500M organic ARR drag. Compounding this narrative is executive instability, highlighted by CEO Shantanu Narayen's announced retirement transition and the abrupt resignation of CFO Dan Durn to join Marvell, leading Wall Street to price in potential multi-year ARR deceleration and gross margin squeeze from rising GPU inference costs.
