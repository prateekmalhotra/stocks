What the Market is Pricing In

At today's trading price of $258.63, the market prices Amazon at 67.1x Normalized Owner Earnings (67.3x EV/OE) and a meager 1.5% Owner Yield. Sell-side commentary across Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, MoffettNathanson, and Bernstein highlights a market demanding flawless execution on AI infrastructure while simultaneously reacting with deep volatility to capital outlays. Management's updated $220 billion cash CapEx budget for 2026 has pushed TTM Free Cash Flow into negative territory (-$7.6 billion). Skeptics argue that funding custom silicon and data center power capacity via $25 billion+ in new corporate bond issuances represents an unsustainable spending treadmill that threatens Return on Invested Capital (audited at 9.4%) if enterprise AI software monetization fails to scale.

Furthermore, institutional analysts caution against low-quality GAAP net income figures distorted by non-operating mark-to-market revaluations. In Q2 2026, Amazon reported $62.6 billion in GAAP net income, but this was heavily inflated by a $53.4 billion paper gain from revaluing its Anthropic equity stake (following a $16.8 billion paper gain in Q1 2026). Excluding these non-cash accounting adjustments reveals true underlying operational earnings facing severe headwinds from semiconductor inflation, rising DRAM/memory pricing, and persistent wage pressures in logistics. The core investment asymmetry hinges on whether Amazon's unprecedented capital deployment will yield structural operating leverage across AWS and Advertising or compress corporate margins under heavy fixed depreciation.
