What the Market is Pricing In

At the current price of $266.73, trading at 20.1x normalized Owner Earnings and 23.9x EV/OE, Mr. Market is pricing in a complex mix of solid macroeconomic defense tailwinds alongside significant structural operational friction. Wall Street recognizes L3Harris as a core Tier-1 defense supplier with an enviable $42.0B backlog, but investors are discounting the valuation to reflect major integration and execution missteps. The sudden termination of CEO Christopher Kubasik in August 2026 for Code of Conduct violations has heightened corporate governance concerns, leaving newly appointed CEO Sam Mehta to navigate a portfolio transition while managing an aggressive internal target of over $1.0B in gross cost savings through the LHX NeXt initiative.

Simultaneously, the market is punishing LHX for delaying its expected debt-reduction catalyst—the initial public offering of its Missile Solutions segment (incorporating legacy Aerojet Rocketdyne operations)—from late 2026 to mid-2027 due to volatile capital markets. Coupled with ongoing cost overruns on multi-year firm-fixed-price development contracts, a heavy $560M annual net interest drag from $11.0B in gross debt, and aggressive market-share acquisition by software-first commercial defense entrants like Anduril and Palantir, Mr. Market is demanding a higher risk premium before re-rating the stock to historical premium multiples.
