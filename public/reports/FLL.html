What the Market is Pricing In

Mr. Market is currently pricing in a highly distressed capital structure and severe terminal risk for Full House Resorts. At $2.06 per share (~$76M equity market capitalization), the market focuses almost exclusively on the company's $475.0M gross debt balance ($450M of 8.25% Senior Secured Notes due 2028 plus $25M revolver draw) against a net debt load of $-442.0M ($-12.06 per share). With annualized cash interest expense running at ~$43.0M, trailing interest coverage hovers near a precarious 1.1x–1.2x. Investors fear a looming refinancing wall in 2028 where elevated interest rates could wipe out equity value or force a highly dilutive restructuring.

Furthermore, the market is pricing in deep operational skepticism regarding Chamonix in Cripple Creek, CO, where win per position per day (WPPD) has lagged at roughly half of local market competitor Monarch Casino Resort in Black Hawk. This underperformance, combined with the delayed financing and complex legal execution required for the ~$302M permanent American Place facility in Waukegan, IL, leads the market to treat FLL equity as a distressed, out-of-the-money call option. Investors assume that property cash flows will be entirely consumed by interest expense and debt service, leaving zero residual equity value.
