Section 3: Financial Audit & Real Cash Flow Mechanics (FY 2025 & Q1 2026)

A rigorous fundamental evaluation of JD.com requires cutting through non-GAAP adjustments to inspect the underlying engine of cash creation. E-commerce giants often highlight non-GAAP net income or operational EBITDA, but long-term shareholder value is governed strictly by cash brought into the business minus required capital expenditures and equity dilution.

In this audit, Stock-Based Compensation (SBC) is treated not as an abstract accounting entry, but as a direct cash-equivalent cost. Issuing equity to employees dilutes existing shareholders; replacing that dilution through share buybacks requires hard cash. Therefore, our Real Cash Flow (RCF) metric is defined as:

Real Cash Flow (RCF) = Operating Cash Flow (OCF) − Net Capital Expenditures − Stock-Based Compensation (SBC)

GAAP Net Income reached RMB 19.6 billion ($2.8 billion), while Non-GAAP Net Income stood at RMB 27.0 billion ($3.9 billion).
