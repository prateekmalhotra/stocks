3. Warren Buffett Owner Earnings & Intrinsic Value Framework

Grounded strictly in Warren Buffett’s 1986 Shareholder Letter methodology, intrinsic value is the discounted present value of expected cash that can be extracted from the business over its remaining life, adjusted for capital expenditure needs and structural dilution.

The 7-Pillar Owner Earnings Calculation:
Owner Earnings = Operating Cash Flow ($858M Q1 Run-Rate) - Maintenance CapEx ($12M) - SBC ($0.0M) + Interest/Dividend Float Income

1. Stock-Based Compensation (SBC) Reality: Constellation charges $0 in diluted SBC expense because executive cash bonuses are legally required to be invested directly into open-market share purchases, resulting in zero share dilution (<0.1% YoY share count delta).
2. Maintenance vs. Growth CapEx: Software platforms require minimal physical capital reinvestment (<1% of revenue). All R&D and product updates are expensed immediately through the income statement.
3. Localized Sovereign Discounting: Grounded in the Canada 10-Year Government Bond Yield of ~3.69% - 3.75%. Adding a standardized equity risk premium of 5.5% yields a hurdle discount rate of 9.25%.

Constellation’s initial Owner Earnings cash yield sits at a robust ~6.5% relative to normalized earnings capacity. When combined with a conservative 10%–12% annual compounding rate from M&A reinvestment at >20% hurdle rates, the intrinsic fair value bridge indicates substantial undervaluation.
