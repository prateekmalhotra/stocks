01

What the Market is Pricing In

At today's price of $143.48 (17.9x P/OE, 16.7x EV/OE), Mr. Market is pricing in steady defensive cash flow stability, but underestimating severe underlying volume headwinds in PepsiCo's highest-margin core divisions: PepsiCo Foods North America (Frito-Lay) and PepsiCo Beverages North America. Following years of aggressive price increases that expanded top-line growth while testing consumer elasticity, unit volume growth in North America has stalled at 0%. Skeptics contend that cumulative inflation has driven consumers across mass retail and convenience channels to trade down to private-label alternatives or reduce snacking frequency altogether.

Furthermore, the market is discounting emerging structural adoption of GLP-1 weight-loss medications, which directly curb snacking frequency and high-calorie beverage intake. To defend market share, management has been forced to step up promotional discounting, roll out smaller portion packaging, and increase marketing expenditures, compressing gross margins by 100 basis points YoY down to 54.0%. The current market valuation assumes historical defensive multiples can be sustained, despite operating earnings relying on temporary tariff refund credits and promotional spending to hit lower-end full-year guidance.
