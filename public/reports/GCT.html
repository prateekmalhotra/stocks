Section 2: Platform Mechanics, Logistics Infrastructure & Competitive Moat

Supplier Fulfilled Retailing (SFR®) Ecosystem Architecture

GigaCloud's proprietary Supplier Fulfilled Retailing (SFR®) model solves structural inefficiencies in traditional big-and-bulky supply chains. Historically, overseas furniture manufacturers (1P suppliers) relied on multi-tiered networks of importers, wholesalers, and freight brokers, incurring heavy inventory carrying risks, fragmented distribution markups, and unpredictable ocean logistics costs.

Under the SFR® engine, GigaCloud integrates product discovery, digital transaction clearing, international ocean transportation, warehousing, and last-mile fulfillment into a unified software-driven marketplace. Asian manufacturers gain direct B2B exposure to thousands of Western resellers without establishing physical overseas operations. Concurrently, Western resellers access cross-border inventory on a zero-inventory-risk basis, selling directly to end consumers while leveraging GigaCloud's flat-rate nationwide delivery network.

1P vs. 3P Revenue Mix & Marketplace Network Dynamics

A key strategic thesis for GigaCloud is the accelerated shift toward third-party (3P) seller services, which drives higher-margin recurring transaction fees, warehousing revenues, and fulfillment margins.

  • 3P Marketplace Scale: Total Marketplace GMV reached $1.745 billion for the trailing 12 months ended June 30, 2026 (+21.3% YoY). Third-party seller GMV expanded 27.0% YoY to $962.3 million, now representing 55.2% of total platform volume (up from 52.7% in prior periods).
  • Active Buyer & Seller Flywheel: Active 3P sellers increased 26.1% YoY to 1,465, while active buyers expanded 17.1% YoY to 12,823. Average spend per active buyer remained exceptionally high at $136,069, demonstrating institutional reseller trust and high customer retention.
  • Cohort Expansion Dynamics: Reseller cohorts display compounding purchasing patterns. Buyers who joined the marketplace in 2025 expanded their quarterly spend from $30 million in Q2 2025 to $99 million in Q2 2026. Pricing Power & Competitive Moat vs. Legacy Players

GigaCloud holds key competitive structural advantages over traditional furniture importers, freight forwarders, and generic 3PL logistics providers:

  1. Algorithmic Load Optimization: Generic logistics networks are optimized for small parcels (e.g., FedEx, UPS, Amazon FBA). GigaCloud's software algorithms optimize cubic spatial utilization in ocean containers and trucks specifically for heavy, non-standard items, reducing damage rates and transportation costs.
  2. Disintermediation & Margin Superiority: Traditional supply chains pass merchandise through 3 to 4 intermediaries, each taking 15-20% markups. By enabling direct factory-to-reseller transactions via SFR®, GigaCloud captures high marketplace fee take-rates while enabling end-buyers to source products at significant wholesale discounts.
  3. Tariff & Supply Chain Resilience: Strengthened customs enforcement and potential tariff fluctuations pose severe challenges to single-country importers. GigaCloud's diversified global vendor base (spanning China, Vietnam, Malaysia, and domestic U.S. suppliers) and centralized logistics scale allow sellers to pivot manufacturing locations rapidly without losing platform distribution.
Competitive Moat Summary:

GigaCloud’s network flywheel creates an escalating barrier to entry. As more 3P sellers join, product selection grows (>90,000 SKUs across 8 major categories), driving higher reseller buyer acquisition. Increased transaction volumes lower fixed warehousing overhead per unit, unlocking carrier volume discounts that GigaCloud reinvests into flat-rate freight pricing — widening its cost advantage over fragmented legacy distributors.
