Section 1: The Premise of the Company

GigaCloud Technology Inc. (NASDAQ: GCT) operates a specialized, technology-enabled B2B e-commerce platform built specifically for "large parcel" merchandise—a high-friction product category encompassing furniture, home furnishings, fitness equipment, and large household appliances. The company pioneered a proprietary operating paradigm termed Supplier Fulfilled Retailing® (SFR®). SFR® directly integrates global manufacturers (primarily based in Asia) with digital resellers and traditional brick-and-mortar retailers across North America, Europe, and Asia. By aggregating fragmented demand, optimizing cross-border logistics, and operating a 35-warehouse global fulfillment footprint spanning 10.7 million square feet, GigaCloud removes multi-layered middleman markups, reduces ocean drayage and last-mile delivery costs, and enables cross-border merchants to execute inventory-light distribution.

Core Business Model & Unit Monetization Engine

GigaCloud generates revenue through three distinct, mutually reinforcing operational channels:

  • GigaCloud 3P (Service Revenue): High-margin service fees charged to independent third-party sellers on the GigaCloud Marketplace. Monetization includes platform transactional commissions, ocean freight forwarding, port drayage, warehouse storage, customs clearance, packaging, and flat-rate last-mile delivery services. 3P GMV expanded 22.7% YoY in FY2025 to $851.2 million, representing 54.0% of total Marketplace GMV ($1,576.8 million). Active 3P sellers grew to 1,299 in FY2025 (up 16.9% YoY from 1,111).
  • GigaCloud 1P (Product Revenue): First-party sales of company-owned inventory executed directly through the GigaCloud Marketplace. Active buyers across the marketplace reached 12,089 in FY2025 (up 29.9% YoY from 9,306), with an average annual spend per buyer of $130,431.
  • Off-Platform E-Commerce & Retail (Product Revenue): Direct sales of first-party inventory to and through third-party e-commerce channels (Amazon, Wayfair, Walmart, Home Depot) and physical retail store networks. This channel expanded following strategic bolt-on acquisitions, including Noble House (indoor/outdoor furniture), Wondersign (interactive digital catalog/POS software for physical furniture stores), and New Classic Home Furnishings (acquired Jan 1, 2026).

Governance, Executive Control & C-Suite Audit

GigaCloud Technology Inc. is incorporated in the Cayman Islands and operates under a dual-class equity voting structure:

  • Dual-Class Capital Structure: Class A ordinary shares carry 1 vote per share, whereas Class B ordinary shares carry 10 votes per share. Founder, Chairman, and CEO Larry Lei Wu holds 100% of the 7.28 million Class B ordinary shares as of Dec 31, 2025, exerting 71.1% of total voting control. The company qualifies as a "Controlled Company" under Nasdaq listing rules.
  • C-Suite Key-Person Stability & Operational Mandate: CEO Larry Wu has led the enterprise since founding in 2006, maintaining long-term strategic continuity. Early venture capital backer DCM systematically reduced its stake post-IPO through public markets without board disruption. Institutional ownership of Class A ordinary shares exceeds 73%.
  • Activist & Short-Seller Audit: There are no active 13D activist campaigns (such as Elliott Management or Starboard Value). Historical short-seller reports (published in 2023–2024 by Culper Research and Grizzly Research alleging undisclosed related-party transactions and phantom warehouse space) were formally addressed by management through detailed SEC disclosures, public warehouse video tours, independent verification by auditor KPMG Huazhen LLP, and robust ongoing operating cash flows.
