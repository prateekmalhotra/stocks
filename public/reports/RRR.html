What the Market is Pricing In

At the current market price of $49.26 (13.3x P/OE, 22.2x EV/OE), Mr. Market is expressing significant skepticism regarding Red Rock Resorts' ability to sustain its historical margin premium while executing a heavy, debt-fueled capital expansion cycle. The market's immediate unease was catalyzed by the October 21, 2026 SEC Form 8-K filing, which disclosed an incremental $500 million Term B loan facility at Term SOFR plus 2.00%. While structured as a balance sheet duration maneuver to pay down drawn revolving credit and fund ongoing development, institutional desks view the debt expansion with caution. Net debt stands at -$3,443M (-$32.79 per share), pushing leverage above 4.0x annualized Adjusted EBITDA at a time when Q2 2026 revenue contracted 3.0% year-over-year and consolidated Adjusted EBITDA dropped 9.3%.

Furthermore, investors are heavily discounting the equity due to a confluence of operational and structural headwinds. Persistent construction disruptions at Green Valley Ranch and Sunset Station, coupled with the initial cannibalization of carded database players from Summerlin flagship Red Rock by the newer Durango property, have eroded near-term earnings power. Looming over these cyclical concerns is the severe labor risk created by the Supreme Court's September 2026 decision denying a stay in Red Rock LLC v. NLRB, compelling the company to bargain with the Culinary Workers Union. The market is pricing in the permanent erosion of Station Casinos' historical 300 to 500 basis point non-union labor cost advantage, suspecting that current owner earnings represent a cyclical peak burdened by high capital intensity.

How Things Are Going Now

Operationally, Red Rock Resorts continues to demonstrate the underlying cash-generative power of its Las Vegas locals duopoly, despite encountering temporary operational cross-currents. In the latest reported quarter (Q2 2026), net revenue came in at $510.3M, representing an annualized run-rate of $2,041.0M with a robust gross margin of 67.0%. Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA reached $208.0M, generating an EBITDA margin of 40.8%, while pure Las Vegas operations sustained an impressive 45.2% margin. Although performance was weighed down by approximately 21,000 lost room nights and a $9M EBITDA disruption from room renovations at Green Valley Ranch, the core business engine remains highly profitable.

The volume drivers of the franchise remain intact. Red Rock's Boarding Pass network continues to anchor repeat suburban visitation, where 76% of carded slot revenue is generated by patrons visiting four or more times per month, and 50% by patrons visiting eight or more times per month. Slots generate more than 80% of total casino win, providing steady, annuity-like flow-through. While Durango has temporarily cannibalized some carded play from Summerlin and Santa Fe Station, it has broadened RRR's footprint across the rapidly expanding Southwest Valley corridor, where population in-migration continues to offset localized consumer softness.

Capital allocation reflects a balanced, albeit leveraged, reinvestment strategy. Management maintains a regular quarterly dividend of $0.26 per share, supplemented by occasional special returns when liquidity permits. Construction is actively advancing on the $385M Durango North expansion (adding 400 slots, bowling, and dining slated for late 2027 completion), while the North Fork tribal management contract approaches its launch to deliver high-margin management fees. The October 2026 $500M incremental Term B facility successfully shifted short-term revolver commitments into secure term debt, providing breathing room to complete the active capital cycle.
