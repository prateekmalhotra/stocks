01

What the Market is Pricing In

Mr. Market is currently pricing Klarna Group plc at $14.33 per share (~$5.4B market cap) under a cloud of intense skepticism, treating its historical growth momentum as unsustainable and its path to GAAP profitability as highly fragile. Following the Q2 earnings release, management slashed full-year 2026 revenue guidance to $4.08B–$4.16B (down from >$4.34B) and cut Gross Merchandise Volume (GMV) expectations due to macro headwinds in its core German market. The market is also spooked by the sudden announcement that long-tenured CFO Niclas Neglén and CMO David Sandström will step down in early 2027, raising governance and accounting reporting red flags right after going public.

Furthermore, investors fear that Klarna's rapid pivot into 'Fair Financing' (longer-duration installment lending) exposes the business to severe credit default tail risks and upfront capital drag just as interest rates remain volatile. With major gateway partner agreements (Stripe, Worldpay, Adyen) being non-exclusive and low-moat competitors like Affirm, Apple Pay, and PayPal vying for point-of-sale checkout dominance, the market is pricing in structural take-rate erosion, ongoing stock-based compensation dilution, and perpetual unprofitability on a normalized Owner Earnings basis.

03

How Things Are Going Now

Despite top-line guidance cuts driven by FX translation drag and German retail softening, Klarna's core operational engine is showing powerful sequential unit progression. The platform currently serves 120 million active consumers (+8% YoY) and over 1.2 million active merchants (+54% YoY), driving an annualized revenue run-rate of $4,168.0M ($1,042.0M in Q2 2026). Revenue per active consumer expanded 24% YoY, demonstrating that engaged users are utilizing Klarna far beyond occasional checkout financing.

The most compelling operational operational reality is the expansion in unit profitability. Transaction Margin Dollars (TMD) reached $446.0M in Q2 2026 (42.8% transaction margin rate, up +450 bps YoY), outpacing revenue growth (+27% YoY) and GMV growth (+18% YoY). This expansion is fueled by high-margin product extensions like Klarna Memberships (2.0M paying subscribers, subscription revenue +600% YoY) and Klarna Card adoption (6.5M active cardholders). Furthermore, net credit provisions improved to 0.52% of GMV, supported by a 10x annual portfolio turnover that allows credit algorithms to re-underwrite risk almost instantly.

On the cost side, Klarna has executed a drastic AI-driven operational restructuring, halving total headcount from peak levels down to ~2,800 employees. This has elevated revenue per employee above $1.24M and pushed Q2 IFRS Operating Income into positive territory at $27M (compared to a $(46)M loss in Q2 2025). While statutory operating cash flows remain lumpy due to loan origination inventory swings, the core underlying transaction economics are compounding rapidly.
